### 1. PRISM Lunar Exploration Program

**Payloads and Research Investigations on the Surface of the Moon (PRISM)** is a major ongoing procurement process run by the [NASA Science Mission Directorate](https://science.nasa.gov/). It solicits and selects high-priority science instruments and technology payloads to be delivered to the Moon. These payloads hitch a ride via commercial landers under the **Commercial Lunar Payload Services (CLPS)** initiative. 

![](../assets/6ae3f7e67e450d5e3172.png)NASA Science (.gov) +3

The primary goal is to build an "interplanetary survival guide" by understanding lunar resources, mapping natural shelters, and evaluating environmental hazards to prepare for the [NASA Moon Base](https://www.nasa.gov/news-release/nasa-adds-new-science-investigations-for-moon-base/) outpost. 

![](../assets/6ae3f7e67e450d5e3172.png)NASA (.gov) +2

**Recently Selected PRISM Suites (September 2026):**

* **LEMS-SP (Lunar Environment Monitoring Station – South Pole):** Led by the University of Maryland Baltimore County, this autonomous station will track seismic activity, micrometeoroid impacts, and drifting volatile gases to map hazard risks. 

  ![](../assets/181049a23035b5a81007.png)X·NASAScience\_ +1
* **GIMLI (Geophysical Instruments for Marius Lunar pit Investigation):** Led by the [Planetary Science Institute](https://www.psi.edu/blog/psi-led-team-selected-for-nasa-prism-program/), this suite will deploy a lander and rover to scan beneath the Marius Hills Pit to find underground lava tubes that could act as natural shelters for astronauts. 

  ![](../assets/47fb7265e74acef2f1e5.png)Planetary Science Institute +1
* **DISCO (DepeLOn-ground Ice and Surface Characterization Investigation):** Led by NASA Ames, this instrument will measure water ice inside micro-cold traps and study how rocket exhaust alters the lunar surface. 

  ![](../assets/181049a23035b5a81007.png)X·NASAScience\_

### 2. PRISM Airborne Spectrometer (Earth Science)

The **Portable Remote Imaging Spectrometer (PRISM)** is an operational, airborne "push-broom" imaging instrument developed by the [NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL)](https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/missions/portable-remote-imaging-spectrometer-prism/).

* **How it works:** Instead of flying in space, it is mounted on aircraft (like the Twin Otter) or Uninhabited Aerial Vehicles (UAVs) to fly at low altitudes beneath cloud cover. 

  ![](../assets/f19702b4e4279fd9f8a5.png)NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL) (.gov) +1
* **Capabilities:** It captures exceptionally high-resolution imagery down to **30 centimeters** per pixel across a spectral range of 350 to 1050 nm (near-ultraviolet to near-infrared). It also uses a two-channel shortwave infrared (SWIR) radiometer for atmospheric correction. 

  ![](../assets/a3a9ce81537d1bee11c5.png)NASA Earthdata (.gov) +2
* **Purpose:** It monitors and characterizes complex coastal zone events that satellites are too far away to see clearly, such as **oil spills, toxic algal blooms, and coral reef health**. 

  ![](../assets/6ae3f7e67e450d5e3172.png)NASA (.gov) +1

### 3. PRISM Supercomputing Cluster (AI / Machine Learning)

**Promoting Research In Science using Machine learning (PRISM)** is a specialized high-performance graphics processing unit (GPU) cluster run by the [NASA Center for Climate Simulation (NCCS)](https://www.nccs.nasa.gov/prism-gpu-cluster/).

* **Hardware:** It is an 87-node GPU cluster utilizing advanced systems like NVIDIA DGX and Supermicro Grace Hopper ARM units (featuring NVIDIA A100, V100, and H100 GPUs).
* **Purpose:** It gives scientists massive computational power to run **AI and machine learning discovery algorithms** directly against more than 70 petabytes of NASA Earth observation, climate, and space data.

## Response 2 — Google AI Mode

The **NSA PRISM program** (formally designated **US-984XN**) is a top-secret electronic surveillance system that **gathers international internet communications from major U.S. technology companies**. 

![](../assets/f48ec470e852199e1b30.png)Wikipedia +3

First revealed to the public in 2013 via leaks by whistleblower [Edward Snowden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Snowden), the program operates under Section 702 of the Foreign Intelligence Surveillance Act (FISA). 

![](../assets/f48ec470e852199e1b30.png)Wikipedia +2

The primary mechanics, capabilities, and controversies surrounding what the PRISM dragnet does include:

### 1. Direct Data Collection from Tech Giants

Unlike other National Security Agency (NSA) programs that intercept data as it flows through underwater fiber-optic cables (known as Upstream collection), PRISM requests data directly from the servers of U.S. internet service providers. The leaked documents named nine major participating tech companies: 

![](../assets/2cb91b5d653148b2d141.png)American Civil Liberties Union +4

* Microsoft, Yahoo!, Google, Facebook, PalTalk, YouTube, Skype, AOL, and Apple. 

  ![](../assets/8da8d91920b7cf0bd8b2.png)National Security Archive

Through secret directives authorized by the [Foreign Intelligence Surveillance Court (FISC)](https://www.fisc.uscourts.gov/), these companies are compelled to hand over data requested by intelligence analysts. 

![](../assets/f48ec470e852199e1b30.png)Wikipedia +1

### 2. Comprehensive Communications Monitoring

PRISM allows the NSA, the FBI, and the CIA to perform extensive surveillance on both live communications and historical stored data. Analysts can pull a vast array of information, including: 

![](../assets/8a082a940467ecebc5eb.png)百度百科 +1

* **Written Content:** Emails, instant message chats, and private social media posts.
* **Media:** Photos, shared files, and videos.
* **Voice and Video:** VoIP calls (like Skype) and video conferencing data.
* **Metadata:** Connection logs, login activities, and target identifiers. 

  ![](../assets/2cb91b5d653148b2d141.png)American Civil Liberties Union +4

### 3. The "Backdoor" Search Controversy

Legally, PRISM is explicitly targeted at **non-U.S. citizens located outside the United States**. Analysts must have at least 51% confidence that their target is a foreigner. 

DNI.gov +2

However, because Americans routinely communicate with people overseas, the program inevitably sweeps up the private communications of millions of ordinary U.S. citizens—a process civil liberties groups refer to as "incidental collection". Once this massive pool of data is stored in government databases, domestic law enforcement (like the FBI) can run warrantless queries using the names or email addresses of specific Americans. Critics call these **"backdoor searches,"** arguing they constitute an unconstitutional end-run around the Fourth Amendment. 

![](../assets/19a435ffbcd3c51f0df0.png)Electronic Frontier Foundation +1

### How it Fits into the Larger NSA Dragnet

PRISM is just one part of a multi-tiered surveillance apparatus. While programs like **Upstream** copy bulk internet traffic from the physical cables out at sea, PRISM acts as the targeted collection mechanism to extract clean, unencrypted communication histories directly from a user's account history. 

![](../assets/f48ec470e852199e1b30.png)Wikipedia +2

The phrase **"FISA scores"** usually refers to one of two completely distinct concepts depending on whether you are looking at cybersecurity metrics or global education statistics.

---

### 1. FISASCORE (Cybersecurity Risk Assessment)

In corporate information security, **FISASCORE™** is a proprietary **credit-style risk measurement scale** (ranging from **300 to 850**). It evaluates how well a company manages its security strategy across administrative, physical, internal technical, and external technical controls. 

![](../assets/1a184c1105257d1eef31.png)Enterprise Minnesota +1

* **What it does:** Similar to a consumer credit score, it translates complex security data into a single, objective number so executives and boards can understand their vulnerabilities. 

  ![](../assets/de862334bb8f9e5efe85.png)Xigent
* **The Scale:** A score of 300 represents severe vulnerability, while 850 indicates highly optimized control maturity. Across all industries, the average score sits relatively low at **557.28**. 

  ![](../assets/1a184c1105257d1eef31.png)Enterprise Minnesota

---

### 2. PISA Scores (Global Education - Common Typo)

If you are looking at international metric rankings, "FISA scores" is frequently a typo for **PISA scores** (Programme for International Student Assessment). 

![](../assets/f48ec470e852199e1b30.png)Wikipedia +1

* **What it does:** Managed by the OECD, these scores measure the scholastic performance of **15-year-old pupils** in mathematics, science, and reading across roughly 90 countries. 

  ![](../assets/11e7df9d700e973b1a9a.png)World Population Review +1
* **Current Standings:** In the global rankings, East Asian economies like **Singapore, China, and Taiwan** routinely lead the metrics, while countries like the Netherlands and the United States sit closer to the upper-middle OECD averages. 

  ![](../assets/7cbc1b9772efd0513c39.png)OCW in cijfers +1

If you are connecting this back to your previous questions about the **NSA PRISM dragnet**, there is no official numerical "score" given to individuals under the Foreign Intelligence Surveillance Act (FISA). Instead, the government tracks **FISA Court Statistics**.
