Response 2 · Google AI Mode
Yes, you are exactly right. The Dutch word "del" matches your description perfectly. 
Ensie
While it is an old word tracing back to Middle Dutch (14th century), it is still widely used today as an informal insult. 
Ensie

Key Meaning & Usage

  • Definition: It is a derogatory term used to describe a trashy, low-class, or promiscuous woman (similar to "slut", "bimbo", or "wench"). 
    Encyclo.nl +1
  • Nuance: It carries a specific connotation of a woman who is loud, wears tacky or overly dramatic makeup, and is perceived to sleep around. 
    Vertalen.nu +1

Fascinating Word Variations

Because "del" is deeply embedded in Dutch street and pop culture, people frequently invent compound words using it to stereotype specific groups of women: 
Ensie
  • Frikandeldel: A modern joke-slang term for an unrefined, trashy woman (blending the word with the popular Dutch deep-fried snack, the frikandel).
  • Canadel: Used right after World War II to describe Dutch women who actively dated liberating Canadian soldiers.
  • Cittadel: A 1990s slang term for a specific archetype of girl who rode a Citta moped.
  • Dérailleurdel: A slang term used in the cycling world to describe "groupie" girls who follow professional bike racers. 
    Ensie
