dichotomy
/dʌɪˈkɒtəmi/
A dichotomy is a division or contrast between two completely opposite, mutually exclusive, or contradictory groups. 
Merriam-Webster +1

Definition and Core Rules

According to the Wikipedia on Dichotomy, a true dichotomy splits a whole set into two parts that satisfy two strict conditions: 
Wikipedia
  • Mutually exclusive: Nothing can belong to both parts at the same time.
  • Jointly exhaustive: Everything in the whole set must belong to one part or the other. 
    Wikipedia

Common Examples

  • Good vs. evil
  • War vs. peace
  • Public vs. private
  • Success vs. failure 
    Vocabulary.com +3

False Dichotomy

A Merriam-Webster Definition of Dichotomy notes that people often misuse the concept to create a false dichotomy. This is an error in reasoning where a situation is presented as having only two choices, even though other valid options exist.
