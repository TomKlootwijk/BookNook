**Testing your basal pH with acid-base strips (often referred to as "beet strips" or health test strips) is typically done using urine or saliva to monitor your body's overall acid-base balance.** If you are looking to track your baseline ("basal") pH levels, testing **morning urine** provides the most accurate and stable measurement. 

![](../assets/7be21a5099e9ba4c25e7.png)Lucovitaal.nl +2

### How to Measure Your Basal pH

To get a reliable baseline of your body's acid-base balance, follow these steps: 

![](../assets/62c64206d97260c1ae68.png)Lekker in mn vel +1

* **Test on an empty stomach:** Perform the test first thing in the morning before eating or drinking anything. 

  ![](../assets/bfa91320ebc61c733ef5.png)Holland And Barrett.nl +2
* **Use the second morning urine:** The very first urine of the day is often highly concentrated and naturally more acidic because your kidneys filter out acids overnight. For an accurate basal baseline, catch a sample of your *second* urination of the morning. 

  ![](../assets/62c64206d97260c1ae68.png)Lekker in mn vel
* **Dip and wait:** Dip the test strip into a clean cup of urine for about 1 to 2 seconds, shake off the excess liquid, and wait roughly 15 seconds. 

  ![](../assets/62c64206d97260c1ae68.png)Lekker in mn vel +2
* **Compare the color:** Match the color pads on your strip against the color chart provided on your test kit packaging to determine your pH number. 

  ![](../assets/9a05b6f8eb450be9efa3.png)YouTube·Med Lab Diagnostics +2
* **Track for several days:** A single test can be influenced by what you ate the night before. It is highly recommended to log your morning results for **3 to 7 consecutive days** to find your true average basal pH. 

  ![](../assets/92ea23afbaedeed165ab.png)www.bol.com +3

### Understanding Your Results

* **pH below 7.0 (Acidic):** A lower morning reading (often between 5.5 and 6.5) is incredibly common because your body expels metabolic waste while you sleep.
* **pH of 7.0 (Neutral):** A value of 7.0 indicates a neutral balance.
* **pH above 7.0 (Alkaline/Basisch):** Readings above 7.0 mean the sample is alkaline. 

  ![](../assets/39722a4c610be7330ad5.png)De Online Drogist +4

*Note: While wellness trends focus on maintaining an "alkaline body balance," please note that checking urine or saliva pH does not reflect your blood pH. Your body naturally regulates its blood pH within a very strict, life-sustaining range (7.35 to 7.45) using your lungs and kidneys, regardless of diet.*
