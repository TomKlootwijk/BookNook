## Response 1 — Google AI Mode

In the business world, a **corporate fall guy** is a slang term for an employee who is intentionally **blamed or scapegoated for a company's failure, scandal, or illegal activity**. This individual is usually positioned right between power and accountability—visible enough to take the blame publicly or internally, allowing higher-ups or the corporation itself to avoid consequences. 

![](../assets/eda6fe05a8638d28efe6.png)LinkedIn·Sudhir Nair +2

### Key Characteristics of Corporate Scapegoating

* **Unequal Accountability:** Junior executives, project managers, or specific departmental heads (like safety or IT officers) are frequently selected because their roles naturally involve managing risk. 

  ![](../assets/b8198132047e1c5b1c0d.png)Reddit·r/projectmanagement +3
* **No Added Pay for the Risk:** Corporations rarely compensate employees for taking the fall. Instead, companies rely on an employee's misplaced sense of "loyalty" or simply use a recently departed worker as the scapegoat. 

  ![](../assets/b8198132047e1c5b1c0d.png)Reddit +1
* **Contractual Liability:** In rare, high-level instances, specific roles like Chief Information Security Officers (CISOs) are contractually obligated to assume responsibility for severe oversights (such as data breaches), which is factored into their high base compensation.

### How to Protect Yourself

On professional platforms and forums like [Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/), career experts and professionals strongly advise against ever voluntarily taking the fall for a manager or executive. If you suspect you are being positioned as a scapegoat, you should take immediate defensive steps: 

![](../assets/b8198132047e1c5b1c0d.png)Reddit

1. **Document Everything:** Keep a detailed, written trail of all approvals, explicit directives, and internal communications. Always "cover your assets" by following standard operating procedures strictly. 

   ![](../assets/b8198132047e1c5b1c0d.png)Reddit·r/projectmanagement +1
2. **Execute Thorough Handovers:** If a project or department is failing, issue clear, fully documented handovers to ensure your specific contributions and warnings are on the record. 

   ![](../assets/b8198132047e1c5b1c0d.png)Reddit·r/projectmanagement
3. **Plan an Exit Strategy:** Working in an environment where leadership shifts blame is a major indicator of a toxic culture. Update your resume and begin looking for a new position elsewhere. 

   ![](../assets/b8198132047e1c5b1c0d.png)Reddit·r/projectmanagement +1
