To get your **sewing machine thread tension right**, the top needle thread and the bottom bobbin thread must lock perfectly together in the middle of your fabric layers. When tension is balanced, the stitches will look identical, flat, and even on both the top and bottom sides. 

![](../assets/9a05b6f8eb450be9efa3.png)YouTube·Pretty Simple Sara +3

Before turning any dials, always make sure your machine is threaded with the **presser foot up**—this ensures the thread sits correctly inside the tension discs. 

![](../assets/7472348877f25352476a.jpg)Sew School London +1

### How to Diagnose and Fix Tension Issues

The easiest way to troubleshoot is to sew a test line on a fabric scrap using **two different colored threads** (one color for the spool, another for the bobbin). Check your results against the guide below: 

![](../assets/b64f829a692513b4962f.png)Instagram·olivia sews +1

| Stitch Appearance | The Problem | The Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Loops on top of the fabric** OR the fabric is puckering. | **Top tension is too tight.** The needle is pulling the bobbin thread up. | **Lower the top tension.** Turn the dial to a lower number. |
| **Loops on the bottom of the fabric** OR a "thread salad" underneath. | **Top tension is too loose.** The bobbin is pulling the needle thread down. | **Raise the top tension.** Turn the dial to a higher number. |
| **Even stitches on both sides.** Perfect locking in the middle. | **Tension is perfectly balanced**. | No adjustment needed! |

### Golden Rules for Adjusting

* **Start in the middle:** Most sewing machine tension dials run from 0 to 9. The default or "auto" zone is almost always between **3 and 5** (usually 4).
* **Adjust incrementally:** Move the dial by half-numbers or single increments at a time, then test sew a new line.
* **Match your fabric:** Tighten the tension slightly for very lightweight or silky fabrics; loosen it slightly for heavy, thick fabrics. 

  ![](../assets/63f6e4d89a10714736ae.png)Tilly and the Buttons +4

If adjusting the dial doesn't fix the issue, completely [rethread the machine](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/g748yWjr7hk) from scratch and clear any hidden lint out of the bobbin case. 

![](../assets/9a05b6f8eb450be9efa3.png)YouTube·Holly Cerise +2
