Gibbs Free Energy (G) determines whether a chemical reaction will occur spontaneously at a constant temperature and pressure. It connects enthalpy (heat energy), entropy (disorder), and absolute temperature through the fundamental thermodynamic equation: 
ΔG=ΔH−TΔS
Where: 
  • ΔG (Gibbs Free Energy Change): If negative ( <0 ), the reaction is spontaneous (exergonic). If positive ( >0 ), it is non-spontaneous (endergonic).
  • ΔH (Enthalpy Change): The heat absorbed or released. Negative is exothermic; positive is endothermic.
  • ΔS (Entropy Change): The change in system molecular disorder. Positive means disorder increases; negative means it decreases.
  • T (Temperature): Absolute temperature measured in Kelvin (K).
