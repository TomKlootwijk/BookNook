More than 600,000 Soviet citizens—including plant workers, firefighters, miners, and military conscripts—were designated as [Chernobyl liquidators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chernobyl_liquidators). They were forced into highly radioactive environments to contain the radiation and build the massive concrete structure known as the "Sarcophagus" over Reactor No. 4.

### The Key Officials Accused of the Cover-Up

If you are instead looking for the specific high-level officials and Soviet leaders who orchestrate the **political and information cover-up**, key figures included: 

![](../assets/1fa705373acb6649ccaf.jpg)Los Angeles Times +1

---

![](../assets/a6fae262e793b972f423.webp)

**Viktor Bryukhanov:** The director of the [Chernobyl Nuclear Power Plant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chernobyl_Nuclear_Power_Plant). He initially downplayed the severity of the explosion, reporting minor radiation levels to Moscow while knowing the reactor core was exposed.

---

![](../assets/875dda989b9eff340136.jpg)

**Anatoly Dyatlov:** The deputy chief engineer who supervised the fatal safety test. He pressured operators to keep testing despite dangerous power levels and heavily hidden reactor flaws.

---

![](../assets/391f1a06fae5a7206182.jpg)

**Boris Shcherbina:** The head of the government commission sent by the Kremlin. He delayed the evacuation of the nearby city of Pripyat for 36 hours to maintain secrecy and minimize panic.

---

![](../assets/da461592d320f34b2ba7.webp)

**Mikhail Gorbachev and the Soviet Politburo:** The top Kremlin leadership. They kept the disaster secret from the global public and their own citizens until Swedish radiation sensors forced them to admit it.
