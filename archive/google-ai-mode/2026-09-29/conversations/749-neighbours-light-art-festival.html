Signify N.V. (formerly Philips Lighting) is the world's largest lighting corporation. Originally founded as part of Philips in Eindhoven, it was spun off as an independent entity in 2016 and listed on the Euronext Amsterdam Stock Exchange under the ticker LIGHT. While the company rebranded to Signify, it continues to design, manufacture, and sell its products under the highly trusted Philips brand license (including Philips Hue, Philips LED, and professional lighting lines). 
www.signify.com +1

Corporate Overview & Strategy

  • The Brand Split: Signify focuses exclusively on smart illumination, consumer connected lighting, and internet-of-things (IoT) ecosystems. In contrast, Royal Philips handles healthcare technology. 
    Philips
  • New Leadership: The company appointed a new CEO, As Tempelman, to steer a strategic pivot away from traditional, flat bulb markets and toward high-margin connected lighting products.
