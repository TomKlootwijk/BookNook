Tsjernobyl people who were forced to cover up the meltdown what was the name
Response 1 · Google AI Mode
The civil and military personnel who were forced or volunteered to handle the aftermath, clean up the radioactive debris, and bury the melted reactor at the Chernobyl nuclear plant were officially called liquidators. 
Wikipedia +1
More than 600,000 Soviet citizens—including plant workers, firefighters, miners, and military conscripts—were designated as Chernobyl liquidators. They were forced into highly radioactive environments to contain the radiation and build the massive concrete structure known as the "Sarcophagus" over Reactor No. 4. 
BBC +1

The Key Officials Accused of the Cover-Up

If you are instead looking for the specific high-level officials and Soviet leaders who orchestrate the political and information cover-up, key figures included: 
Los Angeles Times +1
Viktor Bryukhanov: The director of the Chernobyl Nuclear Power Plant. He initially downplayed the severity of the explosion, reporting minor radiation levels to Moscow while knowing the reactor core was exposed.
Anatoly Dyatlov: The deputy chief engineer who supervised the fatal safety test. He pressured operators to keep testing despite dangerous power levels and heavily hidden reactor flaws.
Boris Shcherbina: The head of the government commission sent by the Kremlin. He delayed the evacuation of the nearby city of Pripyat for 36 hours to maintain secrecy and minimize panic.
Mikhail Gorbachev and the Soviet Politburo: The top Kremlin leadership. They kept the disaster secret from the global public and their own citizens until Swedish radiation sensors forced them to admit it.
