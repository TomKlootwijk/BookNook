Prompt 1 · You
do moire patterns have more to do with the eyes than the larynx vocoder?
Response 1 · Google AI Mode
Yes, moiré patterns have everything to do with the eyes and absolutely nothing to do with a larynx vocoder. 
Moiré patterns are a purely visual interference phenomenon that occurs when two regular, repeating geometric grids or lines overlap at an offset or a slight angle. This creates a completely new, coarser third pattern or "optical illusion" that your eyes perceive. 
Chalkdust +3
A larynx (voice) vocoder, on the other hand, is an audio processing technology. It analyzes the frequency bands of a voice (the modulator) and applies those spectral envelopes to another sound source, like a synthesizer (the carrier).

Your brain only ever processes electrical signals traveling down the optic nerve, not actual photons of light.
