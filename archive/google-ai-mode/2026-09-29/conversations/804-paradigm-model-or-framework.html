A paradigm is a model, pattern, or shared framework that shapes how people think about something, do something, or interpret reality. 
Merriam-Webster +2
The word can mean a few different things depending on how you use it: 
  • A Standard Model or Archetype: It is a perfect template or example that others copy. For instance, a highly successful company might be called the paradigm of modern business. 
    Quora +2
  • A Mindset or Lens: In daily life or psychology, it represents your unwritten rules and mental programming. It dictates how you view situations, perceive problems, and behave. 
    YouTube·Live On Purpose TV +3
  • A Scientific Framework: In academics, it is the collection of theories, laws, and accepted knowledge that defines a whole discipline (like the Darwinian paradigm in biology). 
    Quora +1
When that fundamental way of thinking changes completely—like switching from typewriters to laptops, or changing the scientific belief that the sun revolves around the Earth—it is called a paradigm shift.
