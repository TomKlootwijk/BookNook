Oman
Neighboring Countries
Oman, officially the Sultanate of Oman, is a Middle Eastern nation located on the southeastern coast of the Arabian Peninsula. It shares land borders with the United Arab Emirates, Saudi Arabia, and Yemen. Positioned strategically at the mouth of the Persian Gulf along the Strait of Hormuz, Oman is highly regarded for its rich maritime history, diverse landscapes, and deep-rooted commitment to cultural heritage. Its capital and largest metropolitan center is Muscat.

Core Overview

  • Government & Leadership: Oman is an absolute monarchy (sultanate) ruled by Sultan Haitham bin Tariq, who succeeded the long-reigning Sultan Qaboos in 2020. The country operates under the progressive guidance of Oman Vision 2040, a strategic roadmap aiming to diversify the economy heavily into tourism, logistics, and non-oil sectors. 
    Britannica +2
  • Culture & Religion: The state religion is Islam, with the majority practicing Ibadhism—a distinct school of faith known for its moderation, tolerance, and emphasis on peaceful diplomacy. Traditional architectural identity is fiercely protected; for instance, building codes in Muscat prohibit glass skyscrapers and restrict structures from exceeding 10 stories. 
    Wikipedia +2
  • Geopolitics: Known frequently as a neutral mediator, Oman maintains a unique and steady relationship with regional and global players alike, regularly facilitating critical diplomatic communications between the West and neighboring states.

Key Highlights for Travelers

Oman is often celebrated as one of the region's best-kept secrets, offering raw landscapes rather than commercialized attractions. 
YouTube·Joe HaTTab +1
  • Vibrant Bazaars & History: The coastal capital of Muscat is famous for the stunning Sultan Qaboos Grand Mosque and the ancient, winding alleys of the Muttrah Souk.
  • Oasis Wadis: The country features breathtaking canyon oases like Wadi Shab and Wadi Bani Khalid, where deep turquoise pools are framed by sheer desert cliffs.
  • Historic Fortresses: Nizwa, the former ancient capital, is home to a massive 17th-century circular fort and a historic livestock market that still bustles every Friday morning.
  • The Green South: In stark contrast to the vast dunes of the Sharqiyah Sands, the southern city of Salalah undergoes a radical transformation during the summer Khareef (monsoon), turning the desert landscape into a lush, misty green tropical paradise. 
    Experience Oman +5

While traditionally considered one of the safest nations in the world, regional political developments in 2026 have influenced current safety frameworks. Due to geopolitical tensions in the wider Middle East, major international entities like the US State Department and several European agencies currently designate Oman under a Level 3: Reconsider Travel status.
