Mathematical Formalization

To escape synchronous time frames across relativistic distances, the interstellar civilization models its distributed state as a CvRDT (Conflict-free Replicated Data Type) structured as a bounded join-semilattice. 
A state-based join-semilattice is mathematically formalized as a tuple (𝒮,⊔), where: 
  • 𝒮 is the set of all possible partial states known by any localized planetary or orbital replica. 
  • ⊔ is a binary operator (⊔:𝒮×𝒮→𝒮) called the join (least upper bound), satisfying three fundamental algebraic properties:
    1. Idempotence: x⊔x=x∀x∈𝒮 (merging a state with itself changes nothing).
    2. Commutativity: x⊔y=y⊔x∀x,y∈𝒮 (order of arrival does not matter).
    3. Associativity: (x⊔y)⊔z=x⊔(y⊔z)∀x,y,z∈𝒮 (grouping of arrivals does not matter). 
Because network messages across deep space can be delayed, duplicated, or reordered due to orbital mechanics and gravitational lensing, any two replicas A and B can safely merge their states x and y via x⊔y. This guarantees strict Strong Eventual Consistency (SEC). Replicas advance monotonically up the partial order ≤ defined by: 
x≤y⟺x⊔y=y
