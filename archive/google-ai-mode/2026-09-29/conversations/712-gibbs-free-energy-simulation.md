**Gibbs Free Energy ( $G$ )** determines whether a chemical reaction will occur spontaneously at a constant temperature and pressure. It connects enthalpy (heat energy), entropy (disorder), and absolute temperature through the fundamental thermodynamic equation:

$\Delta G=\Delta H-T\Delta S$

Where:

* **$\Delta G$  (Gibbs Free Energy Change):** If negative (  $<0$  ), the reaction is **spontaneous** (exergonic). If positive (  $>0$  ), it is **non-spontaneous** (endergonic).
* **$\Delta H$  (Enthalpy Change):** The heat absorbed or released. Negative is exothermic; positive is endothermic.
* **$\Delta S$  (Entropy Change):** The change in system molecular disorder. Positive means disorder increases; negative means it decreases.
* **$T$  (Temperature):** Absolute temperature measured in Kelvin ( $K$ ).
