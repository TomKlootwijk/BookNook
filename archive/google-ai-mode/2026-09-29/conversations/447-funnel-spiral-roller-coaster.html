This query frames a fascinating mechanical and physical concept for the topological battle royale funnel coaster. By shrinking the radius linearly over a 1D timeline interval (the "storm ring" mechanics), the physics of the roller coaster carts change dramatically as they descend into the funnel. 

The Physics Breakdown

When an object rolls in a circular motion and its radius (r) decreases linearly over a timeline (t), the outward force (centrifugal force, Fc) depends strictly on how the vehicle's speed is managed along interval X: 
  1. If Constant Linear Velocity (v) is Maintained:
    Fc=m·v2r
    As the radius r decreases linearly toward the center of the funnel, the outward force increases exponentially. Carts would experience crushing G-forces the closer they get to the center. To make the force dissipate, the velocity must be actively checked or drained.     
  2. If Angular Velocity (ω) is Maintained:
    Fc=m·ω2·r
    If the track layout forces a constant spin rate (or the carts intentionally scrub speed), the outward force dissipates linearly matching the decreasing radius.
