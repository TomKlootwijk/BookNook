In the context of **physics and engineering**, a **pinion** transitions from a software name to a fundamental component of classical mechanics and computational astrophysics. Depending on whether you are looking at standard physics (mechanics) or advanced computational physics, a pinion represents two distinct things:

### 1. Rotational Mechanics: The Rack and Pinion System

In classical mechanics, a **pinion** is a small, circular driving gear with teeth that meshes with either a larger gear or a flat, linear gear track called a **rack**. 

![](../assets/48cdb51bcabd5d21554a.png)Chamol Gear +1

When undergraduate engineering or physics students do a **capstone project** involving kinematics and dynamic modeling, the rack and pinion is a favorite textbook system. Physics problems involving a pinion typically calculate: 

![](../assets/9a05b6f8eb450be9efa3.png)YouTube·Engineering Corner +1

* **Motion Conversion:** It converts rotational motion (angular velocity,  $\omega $ ) into linear motion (velocity,  $v$ ) using the exact geometric constraint  $v = R\cdot\omega$ , where  $R$  is the radius of the pinion. 

  ![](../assets/9a05b6f8eb450be9efa3.png)YouTube·Engineering Corner +1
* **Equivalent Inertia:** In dynamic equations, physicists use [Newton's equations of motion](https://www.britannica.com/science/Newtons-laws-of-motion) to model the torque applied to the pinion and determine the **equivalent inertia** of the entire system as it moves the mass of the rack. 

  ![](../assets/9a05b6f8eb450be9efa3.png)YouTube +1

### 2. Astrophysics: PINION (Physics-Informed Neural Network)

If your capstone context is advanced computational or theoretical physics, **PINION** stands for **Physics-Informed neural Network for reIONization**. 

![](../assets/0f65b28847ba5dcd2a88.png)arXiv.org

* **What it does:** It is a machine learning framework used by astrophysicists to simulate the **Epoch of Reionization**—the cosmic era when the first stars ionized the neutral hydrogen gas filling the universe. 

  ![](../assets/0f65b28847ba5dcd2a88.png)arXiv.org
* **The Physics Constraint:** Standard neural networks just look at data patterns, but PINION forces the AI to obey real physical laws by embedding a **physics constraint on the reionization chemistry equation** directly into its training. This allows physicists to simulate vast, complex cosmic evolution incredibly fast without breaking the laws of thermodynamics and chemistry. 

  ![](../assets/a712664b2e27a830312a.png)Infoscience - EPFL +1
