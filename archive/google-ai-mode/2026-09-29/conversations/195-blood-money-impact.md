## Response 1 — Google AI Mode

If someone is said to "touch" or deal with **blood money**, it usually means they are involved with money obtained through highly unethical, violent, or illegal means.

Depending on the context, the phrase carries several distinct meanings:

### 1. Figurative Meaning (Illicit or Unethical Gains)

In everyday conversation and modern literature, "blood money" refers to **money obtained at the cost of another person's life, livelihood, or severe suffering**. If someone touches blood money in this sense, they are:

* Profiting from a tragedy, war, human trafficking, or the exploitation of others.
* Accepting funds that are morally tainted, often leading to a sense of guilt, public disgrace, or corruption.

### 2. Literal Meaning (Hired Murder)

Historically and in criminal contexts, blood money is the **fee paid to a hired killer (a hitman) or an informant** whose testimony leads to someone’s execution. In this case, touching blood money means participating directly in a murder-for-hire plot.

### 3. Legal and Cultural Restitution (*Diyya*)

In various historical societies (such as ancient Germanic law's *wergild*) and under certain modern legal frameworks like Islamic Sharia law (*Diyya*), blood money is **lawful financial compensation paid by a perpetrator (or their family) to the family of a murder or manslaughter victim**. When a family "touches" or accepts this money:

* It acts as legal restitution to alleviate financial hardship caused by the loss.
* It is legally agreed upon by both parties to avoid further retaliation, capital punishment, or a blood feud.

### 4. Biblical Symbolism

The concept is heavily tied to the biblical story of **Judas Iscariot**, who accepted 30 pieces of silver to betray Jesus. When Judas tried to return the money out of guilt, the chief priests refused to put it into the temple treasury because it was "blood money," using it instead to buy a burial field for foreigners. In a religious context, touching blood money represents the ultimate betrayal and spiritual corruption.
