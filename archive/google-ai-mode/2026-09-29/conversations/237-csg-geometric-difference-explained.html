  • The Shape of X: A full cross made of two intersecting diagonal strokes (⧵ and /).
  • The Shape of Y: A V-shape on top meeting at a central node, with a single vertical stem (|) dropping straight down from the center.
  • The Difference: The top "V" of the Y perfectly masks out and removes the top "V" of the X. The vertical bottom stem of the Y passes right through the middle empty space of the X's bottom legs without touching them.
  • What's left: The two bottom diagonal legs of the X (resembling an inverted "V" shape, or /⧵).

  • The Shape of y: A short top-left to center diagonal (⧵), joined by a long top-right to bottom-left diagonal (/) that extends all the way down past the baseline into a descender tail. 
  • The Difference: The long right-to-left stroke of the y completely overlaps and deletes the entire right-to-left stroke of the x. The short top-left stroke of the y deletes the top-left half of the x.
