Ramen noodles
 are traditional Japanese alkaline wheat noodles primarily known for their firm, springy texture and yellow hue. They are typically served in a rich, flavorful broth (such as Shoyu, Shio, Miso, or Tonkotsu) and topped with ingredients like sliced pork (chashu), soft-boiled eggs, and green onions.
