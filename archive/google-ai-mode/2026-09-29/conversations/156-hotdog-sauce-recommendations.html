so what if i just slit one side of a hotdog vertically and the bun is non existant and the hotdog gets heated super heated even (not too cold) and i want sauce which sauce would fit the...
Response 1 · Google AI Mode
When you slit a hot dog vertically down one side and heat it to super-hot temperatures, that slit expands into a wide, canoe-shaped trough perfect for holding thick condiments. Since you are going completely bunless, you need a viscous, rich sauce that clings to the hot dog without running off, balancing the intense heat and salt of a concentrated, seared sausage.
