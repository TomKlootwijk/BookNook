2. Geometric & Vector Abbreviations

Standard notation shortcuts used to compress spatial, vector, and calculus operations in geometric physics: 
  • r→ : Position vector (typically [x,y,z]T).
  • u^,n^,τ^ : Unit vectors (\u for general direction, \hat{\n} for normal/perpendicular, \τ for tangential).
  • x˙,x¨ : Newton's dot notation for time derivatives (x˙=dxdt, x¨=d2xdt2). Highly common shorthand in kinematics.
  • s,θ : Arc length (s) and angular position (θ) used in rotational or curvilinear paths.
  • ω,α : Angular velocity (ω=θ˙) and angular acceleration (α=ω˙=θ¨).
  • ∇ (Nabla) : Gradient operator, used if moving into multi-dimensional kinematic fields (e.g., potential energy gradients).
  • a→c,a→t : Centripetal acceleration (v2/r pointing normal to motion) and Tangential acceleration (\.v pointing along motion).
