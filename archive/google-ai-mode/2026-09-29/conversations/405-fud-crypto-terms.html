FUD stands for Fear, Uncertainty, and Doubt. It is a communication and marketing tactic used to spread negative, exaggerated, or false information about a cryptocurrency, project, or the broader market.

Key Aspects of FUD

  • Definition: A deliberate or organic strategy to create pessimism, panic, and skepticism among investors. 
    CoinTracker +2
  • The Goal: Spreading FUD often aims to drive down an asset's price, suppress a competitor, or create cheaper buying opportunities. 
    Binance +1
  • Common Triggers: Rumors of government bans, security exploits, exchange hacks, or misleading social media posts. 
    www.osl.com +1
  • The Result: It frequently causes emotional reactions, panic-selling, and sharp price drops. 
    Finst +2
  • FUDster: A slang term for someone who actively spreads FUD.

Related Crypto Terms

  • FOMO: Fear of Missing Out. The opposite emotional extreme, where investors rush to buy an asset out of greed and fear of missing a price rally. 
    SoFi +1
  • HODL: Hold On for Dear Life. A strategy of keeping your cryptocurrency through market crashes and FUD instead of selling. 
  • REKT: Slang for "wrecked," used when a trader suffers massive financial losses from market crashes or bad trades.
