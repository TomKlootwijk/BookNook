### 🎰 Casino Overview & Features

Gomorrah is visually distinct for its neon-lit, fiery signs mimicking a volcano/pyroclastic display. 

![](../assets/9a05b6f8eb450be9efa3.png)YouTube·Omega Ordained

* **The Main Floor:** Hosts blackjack, roulette, and slot machines. The table rules pay 3 to 2 on blackjack, and the dealer hits on a soft 17. 

  ![](../assets/9a05b6f8eb450be9efa3.png)YouTube·Omega Ordained
* **The House Ban:** Winning over **9,000 chips** triggers a permanent ban from gambling, though you are still allowed to cash out and use the bar. 

  ![](../assets/9a05b6f8eb450be9efa3.png)YouTube·Omega Ordained +1

The player uncovers a sinister plot where Omerta leaders **Big Sal and Nero** are secretly stockpiling a massive shipment of illegal military weapons. Investigating further reveals a horrific torture room filled with medical equipment, chains, and deceased prostitutes.
