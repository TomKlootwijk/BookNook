**"Eyes Right!" (向右看, *xiang you kan*)** is the commanding phrase echoed across Tiananmen Square during a grand Chinese military parade. As the massive columns of disciplined troops march past the reviewing stand, they snap their heads sharply **up and to their right** (which appears to the left from the perspective of the main cameras or the crowd facing them) to salute the guest of honor and high-ranking officials.

This highly synchronized gesture is a signature highlight of the People's Liberation Army (PLA) goose-step review, serving as an intense reveal of absolute precision, unity, and modern state power.
