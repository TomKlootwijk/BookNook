**"Call to Arms"** is a power metal anthem by the American heavy metal band **Manowar**, originally released on June 4, 2002, as the opening track of their hit eighth studio album, *[Warriors of the World](https://manowar.com/music/warriors-of-the-world-10th-anniversary-remastered-edition-2013/track/call-to-arms-remastered/)*. Written by bassist Joey DeMaio, the song is a fast-paced, double-bass-driven track celebrating true metal, brotherhood, and battle.
