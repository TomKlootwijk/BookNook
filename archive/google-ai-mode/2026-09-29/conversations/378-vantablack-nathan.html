What is the original Vantablack?

Outside of music creators, Vantablack itself is a world-famous nanotechnology material developed by Surrey NanoSystems. It is comprised of a "forest" of vertically aligned carbon nanotubes that trap 99.965% of visible light, making 3D objects coated in it look like a flat, two-dimensional void or black hole. It sparked immense mainstream notoriety when contemporary artist Anish Kapoor purchased exclusive artistic rights to the material, legally barring all other artists from using it.
