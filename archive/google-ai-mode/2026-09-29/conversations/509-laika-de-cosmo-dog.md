Laika was a stray Moscow mongrel who became the first living animal to orbit Earth aboard the Soviet spacecraft **Sputnik 2** on November 3, 1957. 

![](../assets/f48ec470e852199e1b30.png)Wikipedia

* **The Real Dog (Laika):** She was a 3-year-old husky-spitz or terrier mix rescued from the streets of Moscow. The mission was a one-way trip because the spacecraft had no return plan, and she died within hours of launch due to cabin overheating. Read more on [Wikipedia's Laika Page](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laika). 

  ![](../assets/f48ec470e852199e1b30.png)Wikipedia +2
* **The Marvel Character (Cosmo the Spacedog):** [Cosmo the Spacedog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cosmo_the_Spacedog) is a fictional telepathic Soviet space dog in **Marvel Comics** and the *Guardians of the Galaxy* films. Cosmo was created by Dan Abnett and Andy Lanning as a direct tribute to Laika.

## Response 2 — Google AI Mode

It looks like you are referring to the **Yondu whistle** (which may have been auto-corrected or misremembered as "yandex").

In the *Guardians of the Galaxy* films, the blue-skinned Ravager leader **Yondu Udonta** controls his lethal weapon, the **[Yaka Arrow](https://marvelcinematicuniverse.fandom.com/wiki/Yaka_Arrow)**, entirely by whistling. 

![](../assets/7bc5154547aabb3e5d59.png)Marvel Cinematic Universe Wiki +1

### How the Whistle Arrow Works:

* **The Weapon:** The Yaka Arrow is a glowing, levitating projectile forged from sound-sensitive metal native to the Centaurian people. 

  ![](../assets/7bc5154547aabb3e5d59.png)Marvel Cinematic Universe Wiki +1
* **The Control:** By shifting the pitch and melody of his whistle, Yondu triggers a red-glowing cybernetic fin on his head, directing the arrow to fly, change direction mid-air, and eliminate entire rooms of enemies in seconds. 

  ![](../assets/9a05b6f8eb450be9efa3.png)YouTube·Marvel Entertainment +1
* **The Legacy:** Following Yondu's death in *Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 2*, the arrow is gifted to his first mate, **Kraglin Obfonteri**, who spends subsequent films practicing and struggling to master the perfect whistle frequency. 

  ![](../assets/7bc5154547aabb3e5d59.png)Marvel Cinematic Universe Wiki +1

### Behind the Scenes

Actor **Michael Rooker** actually performed the whistles on set. According to an online discussion on [Quora](https://www.quora.com/Who-made-Yondus-whistle-in-Guardians-of-the-Galaxy-2), users note that while Rooker was highly capable of producing the varied melodic and sharp pitches required for the character, his original whistling was digitally enhanced in post-production to sound more metallic and high-tech.
