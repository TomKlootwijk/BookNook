  • Freon / Refrigerants (The Phase-Change Medium): Standard AC units rely on halogenated refrigerants (historically R-22 "Freon", now modern alternatives like R-410A).   • Closed-Loop Coolant (The Thermal Isolation): A closed-loop system ensures that the fluid (whether it is refrigerant, water/glycol, or argon gas) remains entirely isolated from the outside environment. This prevents contamination, fouling, scaling, and atmospheric leaks.
