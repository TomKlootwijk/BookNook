## Response 1 — Google AI Mode

The phrase "Secret Service agents servicing machines" generally refers to the highly specialized **master mechanics and technical specialists** employed by the [U.S. Secret Service (USSS)](https://www.dhs.gov/employee-resources/us-secret-service-usss). 

![](../assets/9a05b6f8eb450be9efa3.png)YouTube·U.S. Secret Service +1

Instead of traditional field agents, these individuals are experts who design, maintain, and repair the advanced electronic and mechanical machinery required for protective operations. 

![](../assets/9a05b6f8eb450be9efa3.png)YouTube·U.S. Secret Service

### 🚗 The Fleet: Maintaining "Rolling Robots"

The most prominent "machines" serviced by the agency are its heavily customized, armored vehicles—including the presidential limousine (known as "The Beast") and tactical units like the [Terradyne Gurkha](https://www.facebook.com/northernvirginiapolicecars/videos/the-united-states-secret-service-multi-purpose-tactical-vehicle-a-terradyne-gurk/1359176482295520/). 

![](../assets/b77201693ba57bcfa359.png)Mental Floss +1

* **Rigorous Qualifications:** All technicians responsible for these vehicles must hold [ASE Certifications](https://ase.com/news/individuals-who-maintain-secret-service-vehicles-must-be-ase-certified/). Because modern armored cars feature complex microchips, specialized electronics, and extensive coding, the agency treats them essentially as highly secure, mobile robots. 

  ![](../assets/0ce8f1aba5d8754cff59.png)ASE
* **Custom Armoring and Tech:** Mechanics work directly within specialized Secret Service garages to modify commercial vehicles. They integrate bulletproof materials, flat-tire-proof systems, and complex defensive technology. 

  ![](../assets/b77201693ba57bcfa359.png)Mental Floss
* **Tactical Integration:** Unlike standard commercial mechanics, these master specialists undergo training in weapon retention and shooting drills. This preparation allows them to travel worldwide and directly assist in protective missions if an operational emergency arises. 

  ![](../assets/9a05b6f8eb450be9efa3.png)YouTube·U.S. Secret Service

### 💻 Cyber & Investigative "Machines"

The Secret Service also employs technical experts to manage and monitor information and communications infrastructure:

* **Task Forces:** Through specialized Electronic Crimes Task Forces, personnel monitor compromised networks, investigate automated botnets, and track digital financial crimes. 

  ![](../assets/aa9aa5c0a00376ae85a9.png)United States Secret Service (.gov) +1
* **Field Technology:** Specialists are responsible for deploying and maintaining field hardware used during protective advances. This includes magnetometers (metal detectors), communication encryption systems, surveillance cameras, and specialized [Unmanned Aircraft Systems (UAS/drones)](https://www.facebook.com/UnitedStatesSecretServiceOfficial/videos/the-secret-service-has-been-strengthening-its-capabilities-in-the-sky-the-secret/1863324867686500/). 

  ![](../assets/9a05b6f8eb450be9efa3.png)YouTube·How-Does-It +1
