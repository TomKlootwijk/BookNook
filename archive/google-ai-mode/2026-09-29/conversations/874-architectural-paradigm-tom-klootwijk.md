### Mechanics of the Self-Referential LUT

 $\text{Output}=\text{LUT}[\text{Input}]$

$\Big(x\_{n+1},\,\mathbf{LUT}\_{\mathbf{n+1}}\Big)=\mathbf{LUT}\_{\mathbf{n}}\big[\,x\_{n},\,e\_{n}\,\big]$

* **$x\_{n}$** : The incoming spatial/state data token.
