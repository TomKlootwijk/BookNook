## Prompt 1 — You

## Response 1 — Google AI Mode

The phenomenon you are thinking of is called a **rat king**. It refers to a rare situation where a group of rats become permanently **joined together at their tails**, which are intertwined, knotted, and fused into a central ball. 

![](../assets/f48ec470e852199e1b30.png)Wikipedia +1

### How it Happens

* **The Culprits:** This almost exclusively happens to **black rats** (*Rattus rattus*) because they have particularly long, thin, and flexible tails with a natural grasping reflex.
* **The Cause:** When rats huddle closely together for warmth in confined spaces (like nests or burrows) during cold winters, their tails can easily cross. If their tails are exposed to a sticky substance—such as **sap, sebum, blood, feces, or frozen urine**—they become glued together. 

  ![](../assets/1235121c4e698d7a83a8.png)Guinness World Records +2
* **The Knotting:** As the rats wake up and try to scurry away in different directions, their panic causes them to pull against each other. This inadvertently **tightens the knot**, trapping the animals permanently. 

  ![](../assets/b8198132047e1c5b1c0d.png)Reddit +2

Because they are stuck in a cluster, they cannot properly move to find food or water, making the condition a fatal death sentence in the wild unless they are found and manually untangled by humans. 

![](../assets/69b27e3df288ddb65dfd.png)National Geographic +1

### Real or Myth?

Historically, many people believed rat kings were a complete hoax or folklore. However, several preserved specimens exist in museums worldwide (such as a famous 32-rat cluster found in Germany). The phenomenon was definitively proven to be real when **living rat kings** (and a mouse king) were discovered and filmed in places like Estonia and Russia. 

![](../assets/f48ec470e852199e1b30.png)Wikipedia +3
