### 🚀 Canon History in the Half-Life Universe

* **Half-Life 2: Episode Two (2007):** The gnome makes his debut hidden under a bunk bed in a communication shack during the opening chapter. Carrying this ordinary 8.5-pound prop across the entire game and placing him inside Dr. Magnusson's rocket unlocks the notorious **"Little Rocket Man"** achievement.
* **Half-Life: Alyx (2020):** He returns in the VR prequel. A magnetic bottle opener in his likeness can be spotted in Russell’s lab, and the physical gnome itself can be found on a couch early in the game. Carrying him to the Vault unlocks the **"Gnome Vault of My Own"** achievement.

### 🌌 The Real-World Space Launch

In November 2020, Valve president Gabe Newell teamed up with aerospace company Rocket Lab to **launch a physical, 3D-printed titanium version of Gnome Chompski into actual space**. The mission served as a mass simulator for a rocket launch and raised thousands of dollars for a children's hospital in New Zealand.

To commemorate this event, Valve added a permanent, auto-unlocking Steam achievement called **"Gnome Alone"** to *Half-Life 2: Episode Two*, *Half-Life: Alyx*, and *Left 4 Dead 2*.
