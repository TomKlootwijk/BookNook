### How it Works

During a normal chemical reaction, reactant molecules must overcome a strict energy barrier, known as **activation energy**, to transform into products. A catalyst provides an **alternative chemical pathway** that requires significantly less energy. By lowering this energy barrier, it allows the reaction to happen much faster and more efficiently. Because it is regenerated in its original form at the end, a single catalyst molecule can repeat this cycle thousands of times. 

![](../assets/fb7abb1a467bdcbd7a0c.png)Department of Energy (.gov) +3

### Core Types of Catalysts

* **Heterogeneous Catalysts:** These exist in a **different phase** of matter than the reactants (such as a solid metal catalyst accelerating a reaction between gases). They are highly common in heavy industrial manufacturing. 

  ![](../assets/ab3c0bcf7c7bfcde4a68.png)Britannica +1
* **Homogeneous Catalysts:** These exist in the **same phase** as the reactants (such as an acid dissolved directly into a liquid solution). 

  ![](../assets/7961fc4923256113dd99.png)Study.com
* **Enzymes (Biocatalysts):** Naturally occurring, highly complex proteins that accelerate essential biological functions—like digestion and cellular repair—inside living organisms. 

  ![](../assets/6f72a9d6ab597c4f291a.jpg)Vocabulary.com +1

### Everyday Examples

* **Automotive Catalytic Converters:** Built into vehicle exhaust systems, these use precious metals like platinum and rhodium to convert toxic exhaust gases (like carbon monoxide) into safer emissions (like carbon dioxide and water vapor). 

  ![](../assets/9a05b6f8eb450be9efa3.png)YouTube·Tender Intellects +1
* **The Haber Process:** An industrial chemical method that uses an **iron catalyst** to bond nitrogen and hydrogen into ammonia, which serves as the foundation for the world's agricultural fertilizers. 

  ![](../assets/9a05b6f8eb450be9efa3.png)YouTube·FuseSchool - Global Education
* **Contact Lens Cleaners:** Many hydrogen peroxide cleaning solutions utilize a platinum-coated disk to quickly break down the peroxide into safe water and oxygen bubbles. 

  ![](../assets/2d042205e25e047f7080.png)Pacific Northwest National Laboratory | PNNL (.gov)

*(Note: In everyday English, "catalyst" is also widely used as a metaphor to describe a **person, event, or condition** that quickly sparks a major change or action).*
