If you are looking for a **"rainbow LUT"** to inject a vibrant, multi-colored spectrum into your videos or photos, you are likely looking for either a highly creative artistic preset or a False Color tool used for exposure checking.

Here are the best ways to get that full-rainbow effect:

### 1. Creative Rainbow & Psychedelic LUTs

For an artistic, vibrant, and multi-colored look, you can find preset packs designed specifically to split light or push extreme saturation across the color spectrum:

* **FixThePhoto "Rainbow Colors" LUT:** You can download a free standalone [Rainbow Colors LUT from FixThePhoto](https://fixthephoto.com/color-lut/download-10) which shifts and boosts secondary tones to create a highly stylized, colorful film look.
* **Creative Assets Platforms:** If you use an [Envato Elements LUT Subscription](https://elements.envato.com/video-templates/luts), you can search their library for "Psychedelic", "Spectrum", or "Prism" LUT packs. These distort your footage to create rainbow light leaks, prism reflections, and neon color-cycling. 

  ![](../assets/e0e630045e0e03ea59d2.png)Envato

### 2. Technical False Color LUTs (The Functional "Rainbow")

If you mean a LUT where the entire image literally maps to a strict rainbow (where dark areas are blue, midtones are green, and highlights are yellow/red), you are looking for a **False Color LUT**.

Filmmakers use these to monitor exact exposure levels rather than for the final creative look.

* **DaVinci Resolve / Premiere Pro:** Most modern video editing software comes built-in with a False Color overlay or openFX plugin.
* **Monitoring Brands:** If you are shooting on a physical monitor, brands like [SmallHD](https://www.smallhd.com/) and [Atomos](https://www.atomos.com/) have pre-built False Color profiles built directly into their operating systems to show a full rainbow spectrum across your live camera feed.

### 3. How to Apply a LUT in Your Editor

Once you download a `.cube` file for your creative rainbow look, you can apply it instantly across major editing platforms:

| Editing Software | Where to Apply |
| --- | --- |
| **Adobe Premiere Pro** | Lumetri Color Panel -> Basic Correction -> **Input LUT** |
| **DaVinci Resolve** | Color Page -> **LUTs Browser** (Drag and drop onto a node) |
| **Final Cut Pro** | Effects Browser -> Color -> **Custom LUT** |
| **CapCut (Desktop)** | Adjustment -> **LUT** -> Import |
