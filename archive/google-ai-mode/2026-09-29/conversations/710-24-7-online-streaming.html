1. The Distinction Between BSL-4 and "ABSL-4"

  • BSL-4 (Laboratory Containment): Applies to standard laboratory benches, glove boxes, or positive-pressure protective suits handling high-consequence pathogens in vitro.

Standard BSL-4 definitions generally assume small-scale diagnostic or research volumes (typically under 10 liters).
