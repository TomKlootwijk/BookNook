The Math of Log-Polar SDF Space

In standard cartesian coordinates, rendering micro-details inside a tiny boxwood sphere forces the raymarcher to choke on tiny step sizes. By mapping space into a log-polar domain before evaluating the SDF, you tile the prayer nut's geometry infinitely into the center of the screen.

// Forward Log-Polar Warp
vec2 logPolar(vec2 p) {

The "SDF-Breaking" Trapped Origin

When you warp space logarithmically, you compress infinite space down into the center point.
