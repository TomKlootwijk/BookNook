### 1. Structural Preservation: Flash Freeze & Two-Stage Aerosols

* **The Problem:** Traditional plunge-freezing methods can take too long, allowing proteins and macromolecular complexes to migrate and degrade at the air-water interface. 

  ![](../assets/d198ae97ee3f4e1d468b.png)MRC Laboratory of Molecular Biology
* **The Aerosol Solution:** New two-stage jet/sprayer mechanisms spray microdroplets at ultra-high speeds (up to 100 m/s) onto cryogenically cooled grids. This creates a hyper-fast **vitrification time (< 10 microseconds)**. 

  ![](../assets/d198ae97ee3f4e1d468b.png)MRC Laboratory of Molecular Biology
* **Time-Resolved Analysis:** In some systems, a pulsed **infrared (IR) laser** flash-melts the amorphous ice for microseconds to initiate a chemical or physical reaction before letting it instantly *revitrify*. 

  ![](../assets/b2bd5b4c9749862505bc.png)University of Cambridge +1

### 2. Infrared (IR) Spectrum Frequencies for Macromolecules

When analyzing the flash-frozen matrix using FTIR spectroscopy, distinct **wave number frequencies** (measured in  $\text{cm}^{-1}$ ) pinpoint the functional groups of lipids and carbohydrates: 

![](../assets/20e616f5876df2e8bdd1.png)Archive ouverte HAL +1

| Macromolecule | Key Functional Group | Characteristic IR Frequency ( $\text{cm}^{-1}$ ) | Diagnostic Relevance in Aerosols / Cryo-EM |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Lipids** | Ester Carbonyl ( $\text{C=O}$  stretching) | **1750 – 1700  $\text{cm}^{-1}$** | Measures lipid oxidation, esterification, and phase transitions. |
| **Lipids** | Aliphatic Chains ( $\text{C-H}$  stretching) | **2950 – 2840  $\text{cm}^{-1}$** | Tracks the packing density, crystallization, and fluidity of the lipid bilayer/droplet. |

### 3. The Impact of Flash Freezing on the IR Spectrum

When a liquid aerosol is flash-frozen into a glassy, amorphous state:

1. **Linewidth Narrowing:** The extreme reduction in thermal motion eliminates inhomogeneous broadening, causing IR absorption peaks to become much sharper and more defined.
