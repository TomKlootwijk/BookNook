## Response 1 — Google AI Mode

In **NHL 97**, as well as several other classic and modern entries in Electronic Arts' NHL series, a post-goal replay can **only be skipped by the player who scored the goal**. 

![](../assets/b8198132047e1c5b1c0d.png)Reddit

If you are playing a multiplayer game (either locally or online) and your opponent scores, they have full control over the replay broadcast. If they choose not to press the skip button, you are forced to watch the replay of their goal, their celebration, and the slow-motion highlights until the game automatically resets to center ice for the next faceoff. 

![](../assets/b8198132047e1c5b1c0d.png)Reddit +2

This specific mechanic was designed to allow the scoring player to enjoy their highlight, but it has historically been a source of frustration among players when opponents use it to deliberately slow down the game or annoy their competitors.
