### 1. When the "Free-Rider Problem" Causes System Failure

In economics, if people consume a service as entertainment without contributing to its upkeep, it creates a **free-rider problem**. When the cost of managing a disruptive audience outweighs the benefit of public transparency, platforms or agencies often end the "free trial" of open access. They shift to a collective subscription model, verify identities, or restrict access exclusively to authorized personnel.
