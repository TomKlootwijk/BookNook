The Shift mechanic completely reinvents traditional open-world driving games through several key features:

* **Teleportation and Possession:** Instead of exiting a vehicle on foot like in *Grand Theft Auto*, pressing a button allows the protagonist, Detective John Tanner, to have an out-of-body experience. The camera zooms out into a top-down, Google Maps-style view of the city, allowing the player to float over traffic, select any vehicle on the road, and instantly possess its driver. 

  ![](../assets/9a05b6f8eb450be9efa3.png)YouTube·minimme +2
* **The Narrative Justification:** The game justifies this supernatural superpower with a clever plot twist: Tanner is actually in a **coma** following a severe car crash at the beginning of the game. The entire open world is a hallucination taking place inside his head, explaining why he can magically hop between bodies. When zooming out to Shift, the background audio fades and is replaced by the faint sound of a hospital heart monitor. 

  ![](../assets/a529e3aa389dcbd46a2b.png)Steemit +1
* **Tactical Mission Design:** Players must use the mechanic creatively to solve puzzles and win events. For example, if you need to finish 1st *and* 2nd in a team street race, you can constantly swap back and forth between two different cars to ensure they both cross the line ahead of the pack. 

  ![](../assets/b8198132047e1c5b1c0d.png)Reddit·UltraViolet +2
* **Weapons-Grade Traffic:** Instead of just racing, you can Shift into an oncoming semi-truck or bus half a mile away and deliberately steer it directly into the path of a target you are chasing. 

  ![](../assets/9a05b6f8eb450be9efa3.png)YouTube·GameSpot +1
* **Passenger Reactions:** When Tanner possesses a driver, he inherits the vehicle's original passenger. Driving erratically triggers unique, often humorous dialogue from passengers who are terrified or confused by their friend's sudden, reckless change in behavior. 

  ![](../assets/9a05b6f8eb450be9efa3.png)YouTube·minimme +1

Technically, the mechanic was highly praised upon release because it allowed for instant car swaps across a massive map seamlessly, without any loading screens.
