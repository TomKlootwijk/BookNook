**Alexei Navalny** (June 4, 1976 – February 16, 2024) was a prominent **Russian opposition leader**, lawyer, and anti-corruption activist. He established himself as the most vocal domestic critic of Russian President Vladimir Putin and the ruling United Russia party, which he famously dubbed the "party of crooks and thieves". Navalny utilized digital platforms, notably his YouTube channel with over six million subscribers, to publish extensive investigations exposing high-level Kremlin corruption. 

![](../assets/f48ec470e852199e1b30.png)Wikipedia +3

### Political Activities & Repression

* **Anti-Corruption Campaigns:** In 2011, Navalny founded the [Anti-Corruption Foundation (FBK)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-Corruption_Foundation), an NGO funded by public donations that targeted elite figures like Dmitry Medvedev. 

  ![](../assets/f48ec470e852199e1b30.png)Wikipedia +2
* **Electoral Impact:** He shook Russian politics by capturing **27% of the vote** during the 2013 Moscow mayoral election. Although barred from competing in the 2018 presidential race, his "Smart Voting" strategy actively organized opposition votes against Kremlin-backed candidates. 

  ![](../assets/9a05b6f8eb450be9efa3.png)YouTube·BBC News +3
* **2020 Poisoning:** In August 2020, Navalny survived a near-fatal assassination attempt when he was poisoned with the military-grade nerve agent Novichok during a trip to Siberia. After receiving life-saving treatment in Berlin, he chose to return to Russia in January 2021. He was immediately detained upon arrival at the airport. 

  ![](../assets/f48ec470e852199e1b30.png)Wikipedia +2

### Imprisonment and Death

Navalny was handed multiple politically motivated sentences totaling over 19 years, eventually being moved to the isolated "Polar Wolf" maximum-security penal colony (IK-3) above the Arctic Circle. He died there unexpectedly on **February 16, 2024**, at the age of 47. He is buried at the Borisov Cemetery in Moscow. 

![](../assets/f48ec470e852199e1b30.png)Wikipedia +3

### Forensic Investigation Into Cause of Death

While Russian prison authorities initially claimed Navalny died of natural causes, a joint scientific investigation published by five European nations (the UK, France, Germany, the Netherlands, and Sweden) concluded that he was assassinated. 

![](../assets/b095ae2d9ff0b3ab6986.png)Facebook·CNN +2

Laboratory testing of material samples retrieved from his body revealed lethal traces of **epibatidine**, a highly potent toxin found in the skin of South American poison dart frogs. Because captive frogs do not yield this toxin and the species is non-native to Russia, investigators ruled out accidental or natural exposure. The [UK Foreign Office](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/uk-confirmsrussia-poisoned-navalny-in-prison-with-rare-toxin) and its allies issued formal determinations holding the Russian government responsible, asserting that only the state apparatus possessed the motivation and opportunity to deploy such a rare substance inside a maximum-security prison. 

![](../assets/ab3c0bcf7c7bfcde4a68.png)Britannica +2
