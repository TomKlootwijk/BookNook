The equation 5 × 5 = 25 perfectly represents the total number of points found across 5 pentagrams. 
Because a single pentagram is a regular five-pointed star polygon, it inherently possesses 5 points (or tips). 
Wikipedia +1
If you multiply those 5 points per star by 5 individual pentagrams, you get a total of 25 points.
