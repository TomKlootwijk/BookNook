Yes, flash-frozen peas are vastly superior to bottled (or canned) peas in terms of taste, texture, and nutritional value. The stark contrast between the two comes down to how they are processed and preserved. 
Quora +2

Why Flash-Frozen Peas Win

  • Locked-in Sweetness and Texture: Peas begin converting their natural sugars into bland starch the moment they are picked. Flash-freezing happens within hours of harvest, halting this process instantly. This leaves the peas sweet, tender, and structurally intact with a pleasant "pop" when you bite into them. 
    Nature's Produce +3
  • Higher Nutrient Retention: Because flash-freezing pauses nutrient degradation immediately, frozen peas retain high levels of vitamin C, B vitamins, and antioxidants. 
    YouTube·Ralston D'Souza +1
  • Vibrant Presentation: Frozen peas retain their bright green chlorophyll, adding visual appeal to dishes. 
    Bon Appétit: Recipes, Cooking, Entertaining, Restaurants | Bon Appétit +1

The Downside of Bottled and Canned Peas

  • Overcooked and Mushy: To make bottled or canned peas shelf-stable, they must be heated well beyond boiling temperatures inside the container to ensure sterilization. This prolonged intense heat essentially overcooks the peas, destroying their cellular structure and turning them mushy.
  • Loss of Flavor and Color: The high-heat preservation process dulls their natural sweetness, frequently resulting in a starchy, metallic, or drab flavor. They also lose their bright color, turning a muted, olive-drab green.
  • Leached Nutrients: Water-soluble vitamins leach out of the peas into the surrounding packing liquid, which is typically drained away before eating.
  • Added Sodium: Bottled and canned peas often use salt as a preservative, significantly increasing their sodium content compared to plain frozen peas.
