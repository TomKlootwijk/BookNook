### How Creators Get Paid Without a Credit Card

When it is time to withdraw your earnings, you do not need a credit card. Patreon pays out creators through digital wallets and financial services:

* **PayPal:** You can link a [PayPal Account](https://www.paypal.com) to receive your payouts. Once the funds are in your PayPal balance, you can use them for online shopping or transfer them directly to a standard bank account.
* **Payoneer:** If PayPal is unavailable in your country or you prefer an alternative, you can link a [Payoneer Account](https://www.payoneer.com). Payoneer allows you to withdraw your Patreon funds to a local bank account or use a prepaid debit card.

### Important Risks to Keep in Mind

* **Avoid Using the Balance as a Bank:** It is highly recommended that you routinely withdraw your funds to PayPal or Payoneer. Keeping a large balance directly on Patreon carries risks; if your creator account is ever frozen or flagged for a rule violation, your funds may become completely inaccessible.
* **Age Requirements:** To legally earn money and manage a creator payout account on Patreon, you must be at least 18 years old.

If your original account was permanently barred, **opening a new PayPal account will generally not work.**

PayPal uses highly advanced tracking algorithms. When you register a new account, they automatically cross-reference your **Social Security Number (SSN) / Tax ID, legal name, date of birth, home address, and even your device's IP address** against their database. If they link the new account to your barred identity, the new account will usually be restricted or banned almost immediately.

The good news is that your past bankruptcy itself isn't what permanently locks you out of standard online financial tools. There is a clear distinction between how bankruptcy impacts PayPal versus how you can still easily get paid on Patreon:

---

### Why You Were Barred (Bankruptcy vs. Debt)

* **Standard Accounts:** Opening a basic PayPal account to receive money does *not* require a credit check. PayPal itself explicitly states they do not shut down user accounts solely due to a poor credit score or past bankruptcy. 

  ![](../assets/a28f31d487686d0dbd83.png)Credit.com +1
* **The "Barred" Trigger:** Users are typically only barred by PayPal after a bankruptcy if they **owed PayPal money** at the time of filing (for example, through a negative balance, a PayPal Credit line, or an overturned dispute). When that debt is legally wiped out by the bankruptcy court, PayPal absorbs a loss and exercises its right as a private company to ban the user permanently.

---

### Your Best Alternatives for Patreon Earning

Since a new PayPal account is likely off the table, you should completely bypass PayPal to avoid having your Patreon earnings trapped. Instead, look into these alternative payout options:

**Payoneer**: This is the cleanest alternative for creators barred from PayPal. Payoneer operates entirely independently of PayPal. You can set up a free [Payoneer Account](https://www.payoneer.com) and link it directly to your Patreon creator dashboard. Once your Patreon earnings hit Payoneer, you can easily transfer them right into a standard, everyday local bank account or use a Payoneer prepaid debit card.

* **Direct Deposit (Stripe):** Depending on your region, Patreon also allows creators to deposit earnings directly into a standard checking account via **Stripe**. Stripe handles the backend payout processing, and standard checking accounts do not run hard credit inquiries, making them highly accessible post-bankruptcy.

### The Solution for Dutch Creators: Payoneer Local Bank Transfer

For creators in the Netherlands, Patreon officially routes direct bank transfers through its secondary infrastructure partner, **Payoneer**. 

![](../assets/fbab1bd095f43ff71465.png)Grey +1

The best part about this system is its flexibility: **You do not necessarily need to open or use a separate Payoneer digital wallet.** Instead, you can simply use the interface to input your standard Dutch local bank account details (your IBAN). 

![](../assets/fbab1bd095f43ff71465.png)Grey

#### How it Works:

* **The Process:** When you request a payout, Patreon uses Payoneer's backend rails to send a local bank transfer directly to your Dutch bank account. 

  ![](../assets/e82be79a07e6a07bc05e.png)Patreon Help Center +1
* **The Fees:** Patreon charges a flat fee of **approximately €0.50 (local equivalent)** per payout for this local transfer. 

  ![](../assets/e82be79a07e6a07bc05e.png)Patreon Help Center +1
* **Bankruptcy Accessibility:** Dutch retail banks generally do not run hard credit scores or block standard checking/IBAN accounts due to consumer debt or historical bankruptcy filings, making this a highly accessible and safe setup for your earnings.

---

### How to Set Up Your Payout in the Netherlands

To get your Patreon page ready to pay out to your Dutch bank account without touching PayPal:

1. Log into your Patreon creator account on a desktop browser.
2. Click **Your Account** in the bottom left corner, then select **Settings**.
3. Click on the **Billing and payouts** tab.
4. Select **Add Payout Method**.
5. Set your country to **Netherlands** and select your preferred earning currency (EUR).
6. Choose the **Bank Transfer** option and carefully fill in your Dutch IBAN and account holder details. 

   ![](../assets/e82be79a07e6a07bc05e.png)Patreon Help Center +2

*Note: The first time you add your banking details, Patreon places a automatic **5-day security hold** on your balance to protect your funds. After those 5 days pass, you can withdraw your funds seamlessly whenever you like.*

## Prompt 4 — You

I got a leefrekening, in the process of ending bewindvoering, wouldn't the municipality be happy that I would be off of welfare as a digital creator?

## Response 4 — Google AI Mode

**Yes, your municipality (Gemeente) will generally be very supportive** of you trying to get off welfare (*bijstandsuitkering*) through your work as a digital creator. They prefer individuals to be financially self-sufficient.

However, because you are currently using a **leefrekening** (allowance account) and transitioning out of **bewindvoering** (protective financial guardianship), you must handle this transition with strict transparency. If you launch your Patreon without notifying the right people, it can cause severe financial and legal complications.

---

### ⚠️ The Critical Risk with a Leefrekening & Welfare

While you are on welfare in the Netherlands, the Gemeente tracks every single euro that enters your ecosystem.

* **The Bank Access Restriction:** While under *bewindvoering*, your main bank account is managed entirely by your guardian (*bewindvoerder*). Your *leefrekening* is only meant to receive your weekly or monthly living allowance.
* **The Payout Trap:** If you link your *leefrekening* IBAN to Patreon, Patreon will send your earnings directly to that account. When your guardian or the Gemeente spots unknown incoming deposits on your *leefrekening* statements, **your welfare benefits could be instantly frozen or suspended** under suspicion of fraud or undeclared income.

---

### 📋 The Safe Way to Handle This Transition

To make the municipality happy without risking your current safety net, you need to follow these exact structural steps:

* **Inform Your Bewindvoerder First:** Tell your financial guardian *before* you earn a single euro on Patreon. Because they legally manage your finances until the court formally ends the *bewindvoering*, they need to know about any potential incoming streams.
* **Report the Income to the Gemeente:** You are legally required to report all digital earnings to your case manager (*klantmanager*) at the Gemeente. In the beginning, the municipality will likely deduct your Patreon earnings directly from your monthly welfare check. For example, if you earn €300 on Patreon, your welfare payout will be reduced by €300. You aren't "off" welfare yet, but you are actively reducing your reliance on it, which they highly encourage.
* **The Milestone of Self-Sufficiency:** Once your Patreon earnings consistently match or exceed the statutory monthly welfare amount, the Gemeente will officially and successfully close your welfare case.

---

### 💡 Your Immediate Next Steps

Since you are in the delicate process of ending your *bewindvoering*, I highly recommend taking these proactive actions to protect yourself:

1. **Do not link your IBAN to Patreon yet.** Keep the account in "draft" or setup mode until you have communicated your plan.
2. Send a short, clear message to your **bewindvoerder** stating: *"I am planning to start a Patreon page to generate independent income as a digital creator. How should we manage the incoming payouts during our transition period?"*

If you are comfortable sharing, let me know:

* **How close are you** to the formal court date ending your *bewindvoering*?
* Do you expect your Patreon income to start **immediately as a large amount**, or will it **build up slowly** over a few months?
