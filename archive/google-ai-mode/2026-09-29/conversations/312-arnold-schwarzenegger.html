Here is how the two break down: 
  • Hey Arnold! is a beloved Nickelodeon animated television series created by Craig Bartlett that originally aired from 1996 to 2004. It follows Arnold Shortman, a chill, optimistic fourth-grader with a distinct football-shaped head, who lives with his eccentric grandparents in a city boarding house.
