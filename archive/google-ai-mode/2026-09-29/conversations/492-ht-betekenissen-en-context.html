Prompt 2 · You
Horizontal Tab, een ASCII-stuurteken.
Response 2 · Google AI Mode
In ASCII and modern character encodings like UTF-8, the Horizontal Tab (HT) is a control character used to advance the cursor to the next tab stop. 
Here is the technical breakdown of the Horizontal Tab: 
  • ASCII Code (Decimal): 9
  • ASCII Code (Hexadecimal): 0x09
  • Binary Representation: 00001001
  • Escape Sequences: Commonly represented as \t in programming languages (like Python, C, and Java) or ^I in terminal notation. 

Technical Function

Historically, the Horizontal Tab mechanical signal told a teletypewriter or printer to physically move its carriage to the next pre-set column. In modern software, it is primarily used for formatting source code (indentation) or separating fields in plain-text data files, such as TSV (Tab-Separated Values) formats. 
Unlike a standard space character (0x20), which always occupies exactly one character width, a horizontal tab's visual width is variable and entirely dependent on the software settings displaying it—usually defaulting to the width of 4 or 8 spaces.

Response 3 · Google AI Mode
VTOL stands for Vertical Take-Off and Landing. It refers to any aircraft that can launch, hover, and land straight up and down without needing a runway, and then transition to efficient horizontal flight once airborne. 
Avy drone +1

The Core Mechanisms

VTOL technology achieves the flexibility of a helicopter combined with the speed and range of a traditional airplane through several engineering methods: 
Avy drone
  • Tiltrotors: Massive rotors spin vertically like a helicopter for liftoff, then tilt forward 90 degrees to function as propellers for fixed-wing forward flight (e.g., the military Bell Boeing V-22 Osprey). 
    Wikipedia
  • Thrust Vectoring: High-powered fighter jets redirect the exhaust streams of their jet engines downward to hover and lift off, then swivel the nozzles backward for supersonic flight (e.g., the F-35B Lightning II). 
    Smithsonian +1
  • Lift + Cruise: Using separate propulsion systems—dedicated vertical rotors that only function during takeoff/landing, and a separate rear propeller used purely for cruising forward. 
    YouTube·Aircraft Design Toolbox +1

Modern Evolution: eVTOL

The commercial aviation space is experiencing a massive shift toward eVTOL (electric VTOL) aircraft. Often called "flying taxis," these vehicles utilize electric motors and distributed propulsion to provide quieter, emission-free, urban air mobility. 
Unmanned airspace +3
Commercial passenger services have already begun deploying. Companies like EHang are operating passenger routes in China, while builders like Joby Aviation and Archer Aviation are actively launching services in the UAE and progressing through final regulatory testing in the United States. 
eVTOL.Travel +2

🏁 Step Requirements by Generation

Because the definition of an "Egg cycle" shifted across core Pokémon games, the base number of steps required to hatch an Eevee varies:

If you are actively moving around on a Bicycle (older games) or a Legendary Ride like Koraidon/Miraidon (Scarlet/Violet), the real-world time to hatch an Eevee is roughly:
