The historic Waag building on the Nieuwmarkt in Amsterdam houses FabLab Amsterdam, the first Fab Lab in Europe. 
Waag Futurelab

Key Details

  • Location: 1st and 2nd floor, Nieuwmarkt 4, 1012 CR Amsterdam (a short walk from Amsterdam Centraal or Nieuwmarkt metro station).

Prompt 2 · You
wetlab
Response 2 · Google AI Mode
The Open Wetlab is a unique biotechnology, bio-art, and design laboratory operated by Waag Futurelab inside the historic Waag building at the Nieuwmarkt. It serves as a community biolab created to democratize and demystify the life sciences. 
Waag Futurelab +1

What They Do

The space acts as a collaborative hub for artists, designers, citizen scientists, and "DIY biologists" to experiment with living biological matter, open hardware, and alternative materials. 
Waag Futurelab +1
  • Bio-Materials & Art: Researching eco-friendly practices, such as creating organic inks, growing textiles, or developing alternative bioplastics. 
    Waag Futurelab +1
  • Textile Innovation: Collaborating closely with the on-site TextileLab to experiment with dyeing fabrics naturally using bacteria instead of hazardous chemicals. 
    Waag Futurelab
  • The BioHack Academy: An international flagship program hosted by the lab where participants learn to build their own open-source lab equipment (like centrifuges and incubators) and grow their own biomaterials. 
    Waag Futurelab

Laboratory Equipment & Access Rules

Because it operates as a community space inside a public-facing monumental building, strict safety rules apply: 
Waag Futurelab
  • The Setup: The lab is equipped with sterile hoods, microscopes, centrifuges, spectroscopes, PCR machines, and electrophoresis gear. 
    Waag Futurelab
  • Safety & Constraints: Only non-pathogenic organisms are permitted. You cannot work with recombinant DNA, hazardous/pathogenic chemicals, or culture human tissues here. All waste is rigorously sterilized or chemically treated. 
    Waag Futurelab
  • How to Join: Independent work requires a formal project proposal, biosafety training, and a commitment to keeping your web documentation open-source. 
    Waag Futurelab

Public Events & Visiting

If you don't have a structured research project, you can get hands-on experience through public workshops. The lab regularly opens up for the "Waag Open" series (usually held on the first Thursday evening of the month), which features beginner-friendly bio-design sessions and ethical discussions. 
Waag Futurelab +2
