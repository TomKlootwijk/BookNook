Prompt 1 · You
was everyone a child once?
Response 1 · Google AI Mode
Biologically, yes, every single human adult was once a child. Human life follows a fixed sequence of physical development, moving from an infant to a child, an adolescent, and finally an adult. 
Quora
However, the question becomes more complex when looking at the psychological and social definition of childhood: 
  • The Loss of a "True" Childhood: On platforms like Quora, users point out that while everyone goes through the biological stages, some individuals never get to experience a traditional, carefree childhood due to severe trauma, poverty, or being forced into adult responsibilities far too early. 
    Quora +1
  • The Philosophical View: This distinction is why literature often reminds us of our origins. As Antoine de Saint-Exupéry famously wrote in The Little Prince, "All grown-ups were once children... but only few of them remember it." It serves as a reminder that even the most hardened or complex adults started life with the same vulnerability and innocence as any youth. 
    thecorrespondent.com +3
