Response 5 · Google AI Mode
Ricin does not have a bitter almond taste or smell. Ricin is a highly lethal toxin derived from castor beans, but it is completely odorless and tasteless. 
The characteristic "bitter almond" smell and taste is uniquely associated with Cyanide poisoning. 
Both substances are extremely dangerous medical emergencies, but they affect the body differently: 
  • Cyanide: Known for the bitter almond odor. It prevents your body's cells from using oxygen. This rapidly causes severe cellular suffocating, leading to dramatic symptoms like feeling suddenly lightheaded, dizzy, confused, or a rapid spike and then drop in heart rate and breathing ("feeling so up or down"). 
  • Ricin: Causes severe cellular damage by stopping protein synthesis. If inhaled or ingested, it typically takes hours for symptoms to appear, presenting as severe gastrointestinal illness or respiratory distress, rather than an immediate sensory taste or sudden neurological "up or down" rush.
