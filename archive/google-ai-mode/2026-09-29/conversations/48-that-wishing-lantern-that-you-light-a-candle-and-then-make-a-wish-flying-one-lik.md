### How They Work

Just like a miniature hot air balloon, a sky lantern is made from a lightweight, thin paper shell with an opening at the bottom. A small waxy fuel cell or candle is secured in the center of a bamboo or wire frame at the base. 

![](../assets/f48ec470e852199e1b30.png)Wikipedia

When you light the fuel source: 

![](../assets/a9192481f1dd6b00495a.png)wikiHow +1

1. The flame heats the air inside the paper shell.
2. The hot air expands and becomes less dense than the cold air outside.
3. This creates a gentle upward lift, causing the lantern to float high into the night sky.
4. It stays airborne for about 10 to 12 minutes until the flame naturally goes out, at which point the lantern drifts safely back down to earth. 

   ![](../assets/7e67fb8e6c43002e09c7.png)Amazon.com.be +3

### Traditions and Festivals

People traditionally write their hopes, prayers, or dreams directly onto the paper before letting it go, symbolizing sending their wishes up to heaven. They are a central highlight in beautiful cultural events globally, such as the famous **Pingxi Sky Lantern Festival** in Taiwan and **Yi Peng (Loi Krathong)** in Thailand. They are also widely used around the world to celebrate New Year's Eve, weddings, and memorials.
