Collet slots (often called slits or elastic slots) are the longitudinal cuts milled into a machining collet. They are the most critical feature of a collet's design, acting like a built-in spring that enables the tool or workpiece to be clamped securely. 
parfaite.com +1

Purpose and Function

The primary functions of collet slots include: 
  • Allowing Radial Compression: As a collet is pushed or drawn into a tapered tool holder by a clamping nut or drawbar, the slots allow the walls of the collet to collapse inward. This reduces its internal diameter to firmly grip the shank of a tool or workpiece. 
    Pingyuan Haotian Machinery CO., LTD +2
  • Distributing Clamping Force Evenly: By splitting the sleeve into multiple flexible segments, the slots ensure that uniform, 360-degree radial pressure is applied across the entire contact surface. This dramatically reduces tool runout (T.I.R.) and minimizes surface marring compared to standard multi-jaw chucks. 
    YouTube·Mike Braddock +1

Single-Cut vs. Double-Cut Slots

The arrangement of the slots fundamentally changes how a collet grips: 
Slot TypeDesignClamping CharacteristicsExample
Single-Cut SlotsSlots are cut from one end and stop before the other.Flexes primarily at one hinge point, gripping mainly at the very front edge. Requires workpieces/tool shanks to match the exact size within a tiny tolerance (0.002"–0.003").5C Collets
Double-Cut SlotsSlots alternate direction (one cut from the front, the next cut from the back).Allows the segments to move parallel to one another. This grips across the entire length of the bore and offers a much wider collapse range (up to 0.040").ER Collets

Maintenance Best Practices

Because they are open grooves, collet slots are prone to accumulating fine metal chips, grease, and debris. 
Techniks USA
  • Regular Cleaning: Always blow out the slots with compressed air or clean them using a brass brush and a solvent like WD-40 before swapping tools. Debris trapped in a slot prevents parallel collapse, causing tool slippage, severe runout, or permanent collet deformation. 
    Techniks USA +1
  • Sealed Variations: For high-pressure, coolant-through machining applications, factories use Sealed ER Collets, where the slots are filled with a flexible silicone or rubber compound to block coolant from escaping through the slots. 
    Sikka Collets +1
