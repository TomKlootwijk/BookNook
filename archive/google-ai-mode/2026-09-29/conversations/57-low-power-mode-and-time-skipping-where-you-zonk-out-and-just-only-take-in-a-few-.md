### Why Your Brain Goes Into "Low Power Mode"

* **Cognitive Fatigue:** Your brain uses a massive amount of daily energy. When your mental battery hits 1%, it cuts power to non-essential functions like deep analytical thinking or forming detailed memories.

* **The "Autopilot" Network:** Scientists call this the **Default Mode Network (DMN)**. When you stop actively focusing on a task, this network takes over, allowing you to perform routine actions (like driving a familiar route or washing dishes) with almost zero conscious effort.

When a group or an entire society operates at a baseline level of irrationality or unawareness, and then suddenly experiences a shared awakening to that fact, psychology, sociology, and philosophy use a few distinct terms to describe the phenomenon. 

![](../assets/9a05b6f8eb450be9efa3.png)YouTube·The Temporal Nomad +1

### 1. The Collective Level of Irrationality: **Collective Stupidity** or **Pluralistic Ignorance**

* **Collective Stupidity:** This is a documented sociological term. It refers to a phenomenon where a group of intelligent individuals collectively make highly irrational or self-harming decisions. It occurs because individual critical thinking shuts down in favor of social conformity, cognitive laziness, and algorithmic feedback loops. 

  ![](../assets/9a05b6f8eb450be9efa3.png)YouTube·Sprouts +3
* **Pluralistic Ignorance:** This is a psychological phenomenon where members of a group privately reject a norm or a belief, but incorrectly assume that *everyone else* accepts it. As a result, everyone goes along with a stupid or outdated idea simply because they think they are the only dissenter.

### 2. The Realization: **The Abilene Paradox** or **The Illusion of Unanimity Collapse**

* **The Abilene Paradox:** This specifically maps the transition from collective ignorance to sudden realization. It describes a situation where a group of people collectively decide on a course of action that *none of the individuals actually want*, simply because each person believes it is what everyone else desires. The "realization" occurs when someone finally speaks up, the spell breaks, and everyone simultaneously realizes they were all being collectively foolish.
* **Breaking the Illusion of Unanimity:** In group dynamics, the moment the group realizes its own shared ignorance is known as the collapse of the illusion of unanimity. A single dissenter or an undeniable reality check shatters the social bubble, forcing everyone to instantly realize the collective error. 

  ![](../assets/9a05b6f8eb450be9efa3.png)YouTube·The Logic Room +1

### 3. The Philosophical View: **Bonhoeffer's Theory of Stupidity**

If you are looking at this through a historical or philosophical lens, German theologian **Dietrich Bonhoeffer** famously argued that stupidity is a sociological problem, not an intellectual one. He noted that people allow themselves to be made "stupid" by overwhelming political or religious power, surrendering their independent thinking to a collective spell. The realization of this state usually requires an internal moral awakening or a catastrophic external shock that forces individuals to reclaim their personal autonomy.
