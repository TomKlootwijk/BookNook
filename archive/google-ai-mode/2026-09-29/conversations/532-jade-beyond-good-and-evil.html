Jade is the 20-year-old protagonist of Beyond Good & Evil, a critically acclaimed action-adventure game released for the Nintendo GameCube (alongside PS2, Xbox, and PC) in late 2003. 
Wikipedia +1
Created by Michel Ancel, Jade broke traditional video game tropes of the era. Instead of being a highly sexualized character, she was widely praised by critics as a realistic, tasteful, and highly capable heroine. 
Wikipedia +1

Character Profile & Role

  • Profession: She works as an investigative photojournalist, martial artist, and a freelance animal cataloger. 
    Wikipedia +1
  • The Mission: Living on the planet Hillys (or Hillis), Jade balances running an island lighthouse orphanage with her uncle-like figure Pey'j (a anthropomorphic pig). To save her home and pay for the lighthouse's energy shields, she begins taking photography jobs. 
    Fandom +3
  • The Conspiracy: She is eventually recruited by the underground rebel group known as the IRIS Network. Armed with her Daï-jo combat staff and camera, she sneaks into government facilities to snap photographic proof that the planet's military rulers (the Alpha Sections) are actually conspiring with the invading alien force, the DomZ. 
    Fandom +2
  • Abilities: In the game, players control Jade as she solves environmental puzzles, engages in stealth maneuvers, fights enemies using Jōdō martial arts, and pilots a hovercraft around an open-world aquatic world. 
    Wikipedia +2

The GameCube Legacy

While Beyond Good & Evil was a commercial failure at launch due to an overcrowded holiday release window, it achieved cult classic status. The GameCube version is highly regarded by retro collectors today and frequently ranks on lists of the top games for the system due to its rich world-building, exceptional story, and timeless art style.
