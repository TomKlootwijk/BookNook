### Finding and Retrieving Lost Sheep

* **Listen and Call:** Sheep are social and easily panic when isolated. Move quietly, listen for weak bleats, and call out or use familiar feeding cues to draw them. 

  ![](../assets/d3558842738f788fc468.png)www.creeksideonline.com +2
* **Handle Calmly:** Corner sheep gently in an open pen or small space rather than chasing them. Grab them firmly by the chin/head or the flank/rear to lift and guide them without causing injury. 

  ![](../assets/9a05b6f8eb450be9efa3.png)YouTube·OSU Extension Professionals
* **Check Health First:** Inspect recovered sheep for exhaustion, dehydration, injuries, or foot rot before reintroducing them to the main group. Provide fresh water and feed immediately. 

  ![](../assets/9a05b6f8eb450be9efa3.png)YouTube·Farming Knowhow

### Care and Management of the Flock

* **Keep Them Together:** Sheep find safety in numbers and naturally look for 4 to 5 companions nearby. Never leave a sheep completely isolated for long.
* **Provide Basic Needs:** Ensure access to fresh pasture or quality hay, clean water, and mineral/vitamin supplements.
* **Protect from Predators:** Maintain sturdy, well-maintained fencing and watch for signs of distress or nearby threats. 

  ![](../assets/bc879d208b00aceeece4.png)Rural Ministries +2
