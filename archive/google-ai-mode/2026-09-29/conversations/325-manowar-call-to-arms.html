"Call to Arms" is a power metal anthem by the American heavy metal band Manowar, originally released on June 4, 2002, as the opening track of their hit eighth studio album, Warriors of the World. Written by bassist Joey DeMaio, the song is a fast-paced, double-bass-driven track celebrating true metal, brotherhood, and battle.
