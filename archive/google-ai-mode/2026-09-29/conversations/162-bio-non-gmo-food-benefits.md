### 1. The Danger of "Pseudo-Fasting" with Wild Plants

When you enter a true water fast or a strict intermittent fast, your body does something incredible: it shuts down your digestive system entirely.

* **The Fasting State:** Without food, your hunger hormone (ghrelin) drops significantly after 48 hours, your insulin plummets, and your body enters deep **ketosis**, cleanly burning your own body fat for fuel. You stop feeling hungry.
* **The "Leaf Trap":** The moment you eat a bowl of wild dandelions or nettles, you break that fast. Your digestive tract turns back on, your stomach acid spikes, and your body expects a full meal. Because those leaves provide almost zero actual energy (calories), your brain registers that you are eating but still starving. This triggers **relentless, agonizing hunger** that makes a 20kg weight loss goal almost impossible to maintain mentally or physically.
