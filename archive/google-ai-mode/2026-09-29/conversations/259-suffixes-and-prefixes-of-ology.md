The word *oligopoly* actually combines the prefix *oligo-* (few) with the suffix *-poly* (sell), so it doesn't contain `-ology` (the study of).

### 1. Suffixes added to `-ology`

When you add a suffix to an `-ology` word, you change its part of speech (e.g., from a field of study to a person or an adjective):

* **`-ist` (forms a noun for a person):** Turns the study into the specialist who practices it.
  + *Examples:* Bi**ologist**, Psych**ologist**, Dermat**ologist**. 

    ![](../assets/6f72a9d6ab597c4f291a.jpg)Vocabulary.com +1
* **`-ical` / `-ic` (forms an adjective):** Turns the study into a descriptive word.
  + *Examples:* Biolog**ical**, Psycholog**ical**, Technolog**ical**.

  + *Examples:* Biologic**ally**, Chronologic**ally**.
