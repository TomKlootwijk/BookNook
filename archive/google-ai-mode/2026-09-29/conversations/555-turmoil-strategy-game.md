### About the Game

Released originally in 2016, *Turmoil* is inspired by the **19th-century oil rush** in North America. The gameplay focuses on managing an oil business from the ground up to outperform corporate rivals. 

![](../assets/fa03056db144663e0225.png)Gamious +3

* **Core Gameplay:** Players bid for land at town auctions, use tools like dowsers or moles to find underground oil reserves, build efficient pipe networks, and transport oil via wagons. 

  ![](../assets/b0351f49edea1de036f7.png)Apple +1
* **Market Strategy:** You store your oil in silos and wait for oil prices to peak before selling, or use natural gas pockets to manipulate the market prices directly. 

  ![](../assets/b0351f49edea1de036f7.png)Apple
* **The Endgame:** Players use their profits to buy town shares at the stock auction with the ultimate goal of becoming the new town mayor. 

  ![](../assets/9ee1077481754cedd8ae.png)Google Play +1
* **Multiplayer Update:** The developers added an online multiplayer update, letting players compete against friends or match with random players on compact maps. 

  ![](../assets/d2fc273c7dae1646e191.png)Games Press

You are talking about **"The One and Only HeatoN,"** one of the most legendary, culture-defining **Counter-Strike 1.5** frag movies ever made. 

![](../assets/b8198132047e1c5b1c0d.png)Reddit +2

Released in the summer of 2002, this iconic video immortalized the Swedish powerhouse **Emil "HeatoN" Christensen** during his dominant era with **Ninjas in Pyjamas (NiP)** and **SK Gaming**. For an entire generation of PC gamers, this clip defined what top-tier competitive CS looked like before Counter-Strike 1.6 took over. 

![](../assets/135cba58085bc180a2cb.png)Liquipedia +2

### What Made This Video Legendary

* **The Infamous Eco Round Ace:** The video features the absolute peak of early 2000s esports history—HeatoN's legendary clutch against the British team **4Kings** on `de_aztec` at the **CPL World Championship**. Starting the round with nothing but a USP pistol, he takes down two opponents, steals an AK-47, and completely shreds the remaining three players. 

  ![](../assets/9a05b6f8eb450be9efa3.png)YouTube +1
* **The "Master of the Spray":** This frag movie perfectly captured why HeatoN was widely feared as the global master of recoil control. At a time when everyone else was carefully burst-firing, he would unrepentantly spray down entire corridors and net easy multi-kills. 

  ![](../assets/9a05b6f8eb450be9efa3.png)YouTube·HLTVorg
* **The Soundtrack:** The video is tightly sewn into the nostalgia of the era by blasting high-energy nu-metal and rock tracks that became synonymous with old-school frag montages. 

  ![](../assets/b8198132047e1c5b1c0d.png)Reddit +1
