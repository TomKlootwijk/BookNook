### 🎨 The Aesthetic & Core Loop

### 🧠 The Sunk Cost Fallacy Mechanic

The core psychological loop of your idea relies heavily on a locked-in progression system:

* **The Binary Choice:** At startup, the user chooses a track: **Gender A or Gender B**.
* **The Progression Gate:** As they click, interact, and collect, they build up a profile—unlocking rarer facial traits, tighter mathematical symmetry, or a higher "progression score."

* **The Result:** Users feel forced to keep playing their initial choice simply because of the time, effort, or crypto-assets they have already "sunk" into it.
