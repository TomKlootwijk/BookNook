🎬 1999 Heist Film

Entrapment is a popular high-tech heist film directed by Jon Amiel and starring Sean Connery and Catherine Zeta-Jones. The plot follows an insurance investigator who goes undercover to track down an international art thief, leading to a multi-billion-dollar cyber heist at the Petronas Twin Towers.

⚖️ Legal Definition

In criminal law, entrapment is an affirmative defense used when government agents or police officers induce a person to commit a crime that they otherwise would not have committed. 
LII | Legal Information Institute +1
  • Key Test: The legal focus is on whether the individual had a "predisposition" to commit the crime. If law enforcement simply provides an opportunity for a willing criminal, it is not entrapment. It only becomes entrapment if the police originate the idea and use excessive pressure or coercion to overbear a law-abiding citizen's will. 
    Department of Justice (.gov) +2

🩺 Medical Context

Less commonly, it refers to entrapment neuropathy, which occurs when a nerve is physically compressed or trapped inside the body. The most frequent example is Carpal Tunnel Syndrome, where the median nerve is compressed at the wrist.
