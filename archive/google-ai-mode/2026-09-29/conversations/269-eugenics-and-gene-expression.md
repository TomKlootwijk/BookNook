### 1. Eugenics vs. Racism in Biotechnology

Historically, "old eugenics" was explicitly racist, aiming to proliferate specific racial groups while sterilizing or eliminating others.

In modern biotechnology (often called "liberal eugenics" or consumer eugenics), the focus shifts from state-mandated racial purity to individual choice—such as using [CRISPR gene editing](https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-020-00001-y) or **Preimplantation Genetic Diagnosis (PGD)** to select against diseases or select for desirable traits.

* **Why it's not strictly racism:** Modern biotech can be applied universally across all races to eliminate hereditary diseases. Its baseline motivation is often health or optimization, not racial supremacy.
* **Why it can function like racism (Systemic Bias):** "Desirable traits" (e.g., intelligence, stature, facial features) are often culturally defined by dominant socio-economic or racial groups. If biotechnology is used to engineer deviations away from a perceived "norm," it can inadvertently reinforce systemic racial and class biases, creating a biological caste system.

### 2. Deterministic Philosophical Conviction & Gene Expression

Your query mentions **deterministic philosophical conviction** and **gene expression derivation deviation**. This touches on **genetic determinism**—the flawed belief that physical and behavioral traits are purely dictated by an organism's DNA.

Modern genomics proves that genetic determinism is scientifically inaccurate due to:

* **Epigenetics:** Gene expression changes dynamically based on environmental factors (diet, stress, trauma).
* **Deviation & Complexity:** Most human traits are polygenic (influenced by hundreds of genes) and highly stochastic (random/probabilistic), meaning you cannot perfectly pre-determine a human being's traits or behavioral outcomes purely through a genetic blueprint.

When eugenics is paired with a strict *deterministic conviction*, it treats human potential as a fixed script. When applied to society, this rigid bias often maps directly onto existing racial hierarchies, falsely attributing systemic socio-economic disparities to "superior" or "inferior" genetics.

### 3. The "Zero-Sum" Equation

A **zero-sum game** implies that one group's gain is strictly another group's loss.

* **The Zero-Sum View of Eugenics:** If a society adopts a deterministic conviction that genetic traits dictate human worth, access to biotechnology becomes a zero-sum competition. If wealthy, dominant racial or social groups gain exclusive access to genetic enhancement, they widen the gap of privilege. The "deviation" of the unenhanced is stigmatized, reducing their social value and resources.
* **The Non-Zero-Sum Alternative:** If biotechnology is used purely under a bioconservative or egalitarian framework—focused strictly on universal medical therapeutics (e.g., curing sickle cell anemia or cystic fibrosis)—it lifts the collective baseline of human health without creating a zero-sum hierarchy of human worth.

### Summary

Eugenics becomes functionally equivalent to racism when it relies on **genetic determinism** to rank human value. While modern biotechnology itself is a tool, applying it with a deterministic philosophy risks turning genetic variation into a **zero-sum battleground**, transforming old racial prejudices into new, high-tech biological disparities.
