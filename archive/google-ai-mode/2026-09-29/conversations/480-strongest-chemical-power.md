However, "strongest" can mean a few different things in chemistry. Here is a breakdown of the chemicals that hold the records for strength across different categories:

### 🧪 The Strongest Acid: Fluoroantimonic Acid ( $\text{HSbF}\_{6}$ )

* **What it is:** A mixture of hydrogen fluoride and antimony pentafluoride.
* **Its power:** It is a superacid measured at **20 quintillion ( $2 \times 10^{19}$ ) times stronger than 100% pure sulfuric acid**. It is so aggressively unstable that it will violently explode if it touches water and will easily eat through glass, requiring storage in specialized Teflon ( $\text{PTFE}$ ) containers. 

  ![](../assets/903b037c08f56eadebca.png)ThoughtCo +3

### 💥 The Strongest Base: Ortho-Diethynylbenzene Dianion ( $\text{C}\_{10}\text{H}\_4^{2-}$ )

* **What it is:** A synthetic, gas-phase organic molecular ion.
* **Its power:** Created in laboratories, it holds the record for the **strongest chemical base**, meaning it has the highest known affinity for snatching protons from other molecules, completely eclipsing standard bases like sodium hydroxide.

### ⛓️ The Strongest Chemical Bond: Carbon Monoxide ( $\text{CO}$ )

* **What it is:** A common, colorless, odorless gas.
* **Its power:** On a molecular level, the **carbon-oxygen triple bond** in a carbon monoxide molecule is the strongest chemical bond known to exist, requiring an immense amount of energy ( $1,072 \text{ kJ/mol}$ ) to rip the two atoms apart.

### 🛡️ The Strongest Material: Carbyne / Graphene

* **What it is:** Allotropes (different structural forms) of carbon.
* **Its power:** If you mean structural strength, **graphene** is roughly 200 times stronger than steel by weight. Its theoretical cousin, **carbyne** (a chain of carbon atoms linked by alternating triple and single bonds), is estimated to be twice as strong as graphene.

### 💀 The Strongest Toxin: Botulinum Toxin

* **What it is:** A neurotoxic protein produced by the bacterium *Clostridium botulinum*.
* **Its power:** It is the **most lethal and potent biological substance** known. Just a single gram of evenly dispersed botulinum toxin could theoretically kill millions of people by paralyzing the muscular system.
