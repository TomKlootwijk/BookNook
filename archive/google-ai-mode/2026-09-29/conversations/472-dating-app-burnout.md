stuff like tinder is a desinthyzation machine where you just dont care anymore

## Response 1 — Google AI Mode

You have hit on exactly what psychologists and researchers now call **dating app burnout** or the "**Tinderisation**" of romance. Apps like Tinder are literally designed like **slot machines**. They use gamification, endless scrolling, and unpredictable rewards to keep your brain hooked on the *act* of swiping rather than the actual goal of connecting. 

![](../assets/94f7c693955dcc24b6f2.png)Medium +4

Over time, this gamified loop causes a deep sense of **cynicism, fatigue, and desensitization**. It strips away human empathy in a few distinct ways: 

![](../assets/cd0b3b90a2555d230efd.png)Aaron Balick +1

* **The "ASOS" Effect:** When you view hundreds of people a day, individuals stop feeling like humans and start looking like inventory. As noted in an article on [Aaron Balick's Psychologies Blog](https://www.aaronbalick.com/post/the-tinderisation-of-everyday-life-when-too-much-choice-backfires), people become something we "try on" and easily discard, much like ordering a dozen shirts online and sending most back. 

  ![](../assets/cd0b3b90a2555d230efd.png)Aaron Balick
* **The Illusion of Infinite Choice:** Because there is always another profile one swipe away, people develop "choice fatigue". It makes it incredibly easy to treat others as "droppable"—leading to rampant ghosting and a lack of investment because you're always looking over your current date's shoulder for the "next best thing". 

  ![](../assets/cd0b3b90a2555d230efd.png)Aaron Balick
* **Dopamine Desensitization:** Your brain gets a chemical hit from the match itself, not the conversation. When the notification becomes the reward, the actual process of talking, meeting up, and building a relationship feels like an exhausting chore. 

  ![](../assets/94f7c693955dcc24b6f2.png)Medium +1

You are definitely not alone in feeling this way. In fact, there is a massive cultural backlash happening right now. Data shows that **Gen Z and Millennials are abandoning dating apps** in droves due to this exact soul-crushing fatigue, leading to a massive resurgence in real-world, face-to-face social events and speed dating. 

![](../assets/9a05b6f8eb450be9efa3.png)YouTube +3

When the apps make you stop caring, the only real fix is to step away and stop treating dating like a mobile game.
