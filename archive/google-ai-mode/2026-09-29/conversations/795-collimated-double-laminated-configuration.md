**Collimated double laminated** refers to a configuration that combines a **collimated light or radiation field** with a **double laminated physical structure**. Depending on the specific industry, this terminology applies to two main fields:

### 1. Advanced Architecture & Safety Glazing

In high-end architectural engineering, a **collimated double laminated** unit represents a specialized window or panel assembly designed for laser-safe rooms, precise optical pathways, or high-contrast projection. 

![](../assets/50f3bad6810cd286dcb3.png)Figshare +1

* **The "Collimated" aspect:** The glass panes are structurally optimized to allow an incoming collimated laser beam or light source to pass straight through with practically zero beam distortion, wavefront error, or stray scatter. 

  ![](../assets/9a05b6f8eb450be9efa3.png)YouTube·Radiology Tutorials +1
* **The "Double Laminated" aspect:** The setup consists of multiple glass sheets bonded together by two distinct polymer interlayers (such as PVB or SGP). This ensures maximum safety, blast/burglary protection, and acoustics, while the specific configuration preserves parallel light propagation. 

  ![](../assets/2ba7f3408576bf70471a.png)www.everest.co.uk +2

### 2. Specialized Optical Filters & Display Films

In electronics and optics manufacturing, the phrase refers to multi-layered films used for directional backlighting (e.g., in high-contrast LCDs, head-up displays, or 3D printers): 

![](../assets/0adb08dcac8376b22891.png)Displays & Optical Technologies, Inc. +1

* **Double-Sided Structures:** These sheets utilize a double matrix or array—such as a double-sided micro-lenticular lens film or a double grating layout—to restrict scattering angles. 

  ![](../assets/fe211bbd78a4d251776a.png)ResearchGate +1
* **Collimation Performance:** By laminating these functional optical layers together, they convert diffuse, scattered backlight into highly ordered, parallel "collimated" light rays, drastically increasing energy efficiency and display contrast. 

  ![](../assets/d6d0b5b2667333ab9f89.png)Yudi Optics +1
