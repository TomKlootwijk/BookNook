# Game Design Document: Project Götterdämmerung (Working Title)
**Genre:** Alternate History Alternate Reality Game (ARG) / Tactical RPG / Immersive Sim  
**Visual Style:** Toon-Shaded HDR Glow / Hyper-Stylized Gothic  
**Core Themes:** Albert Speer Megalomania, Dark Fantasy MMORPG Aesthetics, Dieselpunk Occultism  

---

## 1. Executive Summary & High Concept
**Project Götterdämmerung** is an Alternate Reality Game (ARG) set in a dystopian 1960s where the Axis powers won World War II. The aesthetic world merges the gritty, occult-dieselpunk violence of *Wolfenstein: The Old Blood* with the hyper-stylized, vibrant isometric fantasy elements of *Ragnarok Online* and *Rose Online*, all framed within colossal, imposing *Gothic-Speer* architecture. Players interact with a "found-footage" historical layer while decoding architectural blueprints, occult ciphers, and stylized propaganda.

---

## 2. World & Aesthetic Fusion
The visual identity is defined by a striking contrast between grim fascist dystopia and whimsical, cell-shaded fantasy.

| Dimension | Aesthetic Source | Implementation in Game Design |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Architectural Scope** | Albert Speer's *Welthauptstadt Germania* & Gothic Cathedral Design | Colossal granite structures, soaring ribbed vaults, flying buttresses combined with monolithic concrete, and miles-high towers that swallow the sky. |
| **Visual Rendering** | Toon-Shaded / Cel-Shaded with High Dynamic Range (HDR) Glow | Stark black outlines, vibrant color pooling, and intense, oversaturated neon/plasma glows emitting from occult runes, mechanical piping, and searchlights. |
| **Character & Class Design** | *Ragnarok Online* & *Rose Online* | Cute, highly stylized, oversized gear, exaggerated anime-style expressions layered over dark, menacing armor, retro-futuristic gas masks, and mechanical exoskeletons. |
| **Atmosphere & Tone** | *Wolfenstein: The Old Blood* | Grim, brutal, hidden catacombs, reanimated flesh, ancient Germanic cults, and high-stakes espionage against an seemingly invincible war machine. |

## 3. Core Mechanics & ARG Systems
Because this is an ARG, the gameplay blurs the line between the fictional universe and real-world investigation.

### 3.1. The Historical Artifact Layer (Real-World Interaction)
*   **Speer's Blueprints:** Players receive or discover "leaked" architectural blueprints of alternate-history Berlin (Germania). Hidden inside the cross-sections of the Volkshalle are micro-steganographic ciphers.
*   **The In-Game Terminal:** A simulated desktop environment acting as a resistance workstation, mimicking 1960s computing mixed with anomalous, magic-infused tech.

### 3.2. The Stylized Simulation (The "Game" Within the ARG)
*   **The Isometric Tactical Interface:** Combat or structural infiltration is presented in a 2.5D toon-shaded perspective reminiscent of *Ragnarok Online*, allowing players to execute tactical maneuvers against stylized robotic and occult threats.
*   **Cartoony Brutality:** Juxtaposing cute anime combat animations with the heavy, industrial violence of a dieselpunk war zone.

---

## 4. Architectural Profile: The Gothic-Speer Fusion
The primary setting consists of mega-structures designed by a victorious Albert Speer who discovered ancient, eldritch geometry.
*   **The Cathedral-Halle:** A structure capable of holding 200,000 citizens. Its dome is so vast it creates its own indoor weather system, rain falling from gothic vaults decorated with stylized iron eagles.
*   **The Neon-Stained Glass:** Traditional stained glass windows depict mechanical panzers, gears, and historical conquests, backlit by humming HDR-glow plasma fields instead of sunlight.
