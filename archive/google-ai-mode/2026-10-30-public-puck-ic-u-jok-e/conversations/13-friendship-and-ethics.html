Analyzing a hysteresis loop visually helps connect these concepts to the physical graph. 
The graph maps the Magnetic Field Strength (H) against the resulting Magnetic Flux Density (B). 

The Key Points on the Curve:

  • Saturation (Bsat): The peak points at the top right and bottom left where the material is fully magnetized. Increasing H any further will not increase B. 
  • Remanence (Br): The point where the loop crosses the vertical axis (H=0). This is the leftover, residual magnetism trapped in the material. 
  • Coercivity (Hc): The point where the loop crosses the horizontal axis (B=0). This represents the amount of reverse magnetic force required to fully demagnetize the material.
